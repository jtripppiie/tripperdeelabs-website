Origins support

Place and clear

Drag a tray piece onto empty board cells. Fill a complete row or column to clear it. An energy core on a cleared line also clears its crossing line. Rotate a selected piece as often as you like. Tap Bomb or Zap, then choose a board cell; each run starts with two of each.

Save and resume

Continue on the home screen resumes your saved run. Back pauses gameplay; Back again returns home. Timed Rush stops while paused or backgrounded. Starting another mode replaces the current run after confirmation. Do not uninstall or clear app storage to troubleshoot: you may lose progress.

Purchases and privacy

Open Settings on the home screen for Remove Ads, Restore purchases, Privacy choices and the privacy policy. Purchases require Google Play and the account used to buy them. The game is still in development; live purchase availability has not been verified.

Contact
