Clap Counter Support

Start counting: choose Claps, Sound events or Manual in Settings. Tap Start. Add one works during an active session. Pause freezes active time; Resume continues it. Stop & save stores the result in History. Reset clears the current counter after confirmation without deleting saved history.

Microphone permission: allow access when starting sound counting or calibration. If access is denied, use Manual mode or change the permission in Android Settings. Close other recording apps if the microphone is unavailable.

Missed or extra counts: keep the phone near the claps, choose a sensitivity preset, or calibrate while the room is quiet. Loud music, speech and echoes can trigger sound events. Recalibrate when surroundings change. The threshold line shows the live setting, not a fixed decoration.

Calibration: the active session pauses; stay quiet for about two seconds. Successful calibration applies Custom sensitivity. Cancel or Back releases the microphone. An error does not overwrite the existing setting.

Leaving the app: counting pauses when minimized or the screen locks. A system-restored interrupted session returns paused when saved Activity state is available. This is not continuous background recording or guaranteed recovery after every force-stop/power loss.

History: rename a session, share a text summary after a grown-up check, delete an item after confirmation, or clear all history after confirmation. Back keeps the current counter. Times include hours for long sessions.

Remove Ads: intended release price is a one-time $0.99 USD base, localized by Play. Use the same Google account and Restore Purchases. Pending payment does not remove ads until confirmed. If offline, reconnect and restore. Test previews may show purchases unavailable; they do not charge you. No subscription.

Age group: update the local age group in Settings when a different person uses the device. Everyone receives child-directed ad treatment. Purchases, sharing and external contact links require a grown-up check.

Contact: tripperdeelabs@gmail.com. Include app version, Android version and what happened. Do not email recordings, payment-card details or private account credentials.

Sound graph: the EKG-style trace shows recent microphone levels. dBFS is relative to digital full scale; negative numbers are normal. It is not calibrated room-noise dB SPL.

Reviews: Settings includes Rate on Google Play. The link opens the app listing after the grown-up check. A public listing must exist before you can rate or review the app.

Known limitation: a sudden steady tone produced one false count in synthetic testing. Real-world accuracy is still being evaluated.
