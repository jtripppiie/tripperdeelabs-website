Neon Air Hockey support

Play a match

Drag on your half of the rink to move your mallet. Cyan plays at the bottom, coral at the top. Score through the opening at the other end. First to seven wins. Choose Play computer for a solo game, or Two players to share one screen.

Pause, reset and save

The two-bar icon pauses play. The circular-arrow icon resets the puck and mallets for a new face-off without changing the score. Home keeps your local match; Resume match brings it back. Starting another local match asks before replacing it. Open the gear for sound, difficulty and optional Android screen pinning.

Bluetooth: Host or Join

This feature is still undergoing two-phone validation. Install the same app version on both phones, enable Bluetooth, and pair them in Android Bluetooth settings. Open Bluetooth play and grant Nearby devices permission. Choose Host a game on one phone, then Join a game on the other and select the host. Each player sees their own mallet at the bottom. Leaving the foreground or losing the connection ends the session; reconnect to start again. Your saved local match is kept separately.

Between-match ads

Current builds use Google test ads. A full-screen ad may appear after a completed match when you choose Rematch or Home. Use the ad’s Close button when it becomes available. The next local match starts after dismissal. If an ad is unavailable, continue immediately. Bluetooth sessions close before an ad; host and join again for another match.

Troubleshooting

Use Reset if the puck gets trapped. Keep fingers inside the rails to reduce Android edge gestures. Screen pinning is optional and device-dependent; follow Android’s unpin instructions or use Unpin game in Settings. Do not uninstall or clear storage if you want to keep your saved match.

Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com with your app version, device model and what happened.
