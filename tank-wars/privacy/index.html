Tank Wars privacy policy

Updated November 7, 2026. This describes Tank Wars 0.4.0, the current Android candidate, package com.tripperdeelabs.tankwars, provided by TripperDeeLabs.

Local battle data

Age-group choice, terrain, tank health, turn, projectile state and sound preferences are saved in the app’s private device storage. No TripperDeeLabs account or cloud-save service is used. Uninstalling or clearing storage removes this data. Android backup is disabled in the manifest; manufacturer device-transfer behavior may vary.

Bluetooth play

Optional Bluetooth shares aiming and firing commands, terrain changes and battle state directly with your paired opponent. Android Nearby devices permission is requested on supported versions. The game uses Android settings for device discovery and pairing. Battle messages are not uploaded to a TripperDeeLabs server. Leaving the app ends the Bluetooth session; your separate local save is retained.

Age groups and advertising

The intended launch model is free with between-battle ads and no purchases. A neutral age-group selection asks for an age range, not a name or birth date. The choice stays in local app storage and can be changed in Settings. It is self-declared, not identity or parental verification. Gameplay is available for every selection.

Players under 18, those who prefer not to say, and invalid or missing age choices receive no ads. The app does not initiate UMP consent requests or initialize/request Mobile Ads for those groups. Child-directed and under-age request protections are set defensively. Changing from adult to a protected group discards pending ads and prevents new requests; it cannot undo data already processed during earlier adult use.

For adults, the ad-enabled implementation uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain applicable privacy choices before initializing Google Mobile Ads or requesting an ad. Each launch requires a successful privacy update; errors, missing setup, or disallowed requests keep ads off. Settings provides Ad privacy to revisit available privacy choices or retry. No consent form is intentionally displayed over active gameplay.

All ad requests are non-personalized, request maximum content rating G, and enable Google restricted data processing. UMP privacy signals still apply: non-personalized ads can still use device storage and process data. Ads can appear only when leaving a completed battle for New battle or Home. Unavailable ads never block continuing.

Current candidate and SDK data

Production advertising remains disabled by a separate build switch in the signed 0.4.0 candidate. Review builds use Google demonstration interstitials after the same age and privacy gates. Consent setup, device verification and owner approval are required before production advertising is activated.

When these SDK flows are used, Google may process IP addresses (which can imply approximate location), privacy choices, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and permitted device identifiers for consent, ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Android Advertising ID and AdServices advertising ID, attribution and topics permissions are removed. App measurement initialization is delayed until the guarded SDK initialization. These restrictions do not establish that every SDK component collects no data. There is no developer-operated analytics backend or gameplay server.

See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s SDK disclosure. Clearing local storage removes local choices and saves; it does not delete data Google has already received. Google’s handling and retention follow its policies. No universal legal-compliance, Families eligibility or Play approval claim is made.

Permissions and choices

Internet/network-state access supports ads. Nearby devices access is optional for local play. Tank Wars does not request location, contacts, microphone or camera permission. There are no purchases in this build.

Support and privacy questions

Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com. We receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Avoid sending passwords or payment details. You may also contact us concerning support correspondence.

Website analytics are separate from the app and covered by our website privacy policy.
