Dive Watch: Classic & Lume · Wear OS

Analog dive-style watch face for Wear OS

Dive Watch is an analog watch face for Wear OS, with a dive-inspired dial and everyday watch information. It combines sculpted hands, luminous markers, and a date window with a battery indicator built into the left marker. It is for people who prefer a traditional dial while keeping a useful data field on the watch.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Choose the dial that suits you

Classic steel, Red night, and Green lume are included. Customize the hands, choose a wave-textured or pure-black dial, and decide whether to show seconds or an additional digital time readout. The Classic steel theme has accent-color options. A separate always-on layout shows clear digital time and the date when your watch’s display settings allow it.

Read battery and your chosen complication

The wide marker at nine o’clock is the battery gauge. Its fill shrinks as the charge falls, while the border stays visible. Tap it to open battery status for an exact percentage. The lower circle shows steps by default, but you can choose another compatible complication. That data comes from your watch or selected provider; Dive Watch does not independently record activity.

Install on the watch, then customize

Install from Google Play to a compatible Wear OS watch. Touch and hold your current face, choose Add watch face, and select Dive Watch. Open Customize to change the theme and lower data source. There is no separate phone companion, account, subscription, advertising, or analytics in the face. The face has no network permission; a selected data provider can have its own connection needs and privacy practices.

Frequently asked questions

Which watches can use Dive Watch?

It requires Wear OS 5 or newer. It is a watch face for Wear OS, not an Android phone app, Garmin face, or Apple Watch face.

Does it measure depth or make my watch waterproof?

No. The design is decorative. It adds no water resistance and provides no dive-computer functions; follow your watch manufacturer’s operating limits.

Are all three themes included?

Yes. Classic steel, Red night, and Green lume are included in the purchase. There is no subscription or separate theme purchase.

Why might a complication be empty?

Data availability depends on the watch and selected provider. Check the provider’s setup and permissions in watch settings. Some providers may require internet access to update.
