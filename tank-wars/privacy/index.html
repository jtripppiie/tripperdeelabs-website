Tank Wars privacy policy

Updated November 6, 2026. This describes the current Tank Wars Android development build, package com.tripperdeelabs.tankwars, provided by TripperDeeLabs.

Local battle data

Terrain, tank health, turn, projectile state and sound preferences are saved in the app’s private device storage. No TripperDeeLabs account or cloud-save service is used. Uninstalling or clearing storage removes this data. Android backup is disabled in the manifest; manufacturer device-transfer behavior may vary.

Bluetooth play

Optional Bluetooth shares aiming and firing commands, terrain changes and battle state directly with your paired opponent. Android Nearby devices permission is requested on supported versions. The game uses Android settings for device discovery and pairing. Battle messages are not uploaded to a TripperDeeLabs server. Leaving the app ends the Bluetooth session; your separate local save is retained.

Advertising

The current build includes Google Mobile Ads SDK using Google test interstitials. Production ads are disabled. The SDK may process IP addresses, device/network information, diagnostics and ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement and security. Test requests use G-rated, child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment flags. These settings are not a claim of approved production audience or Families eligibility. No developer-operated analytics SDK is included.

See Google’s privacy policy. Production audience, advertising and consent setup must be completed before release. This policy will be updated if practices change.

Permissions and choices

Internet/network-state access supports ads. Nearby devices access is optional for local play. Tank Wars does not request location, contacts, microphone or camera permission. There are no purchases in this build.

Support and privacy questions

Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com. We receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Avoid sending passwords or payment details. You may also contact us concerning support correspondence.

Website analytics are separate from the app and covered by our website privacy policy.
