Home / FamilyLink Offline Chat Privacy Policy

Your nearby chat.
Your choices.

Updated November 6, 2026. Publisher: TripperDeeLabs. Package: com.familylink.offlinechat. This policy describes the current development build, not a claim of production approval.

No developer-operated chat server

FamilyLink connects nearby devices using Bluetooth or a local Wi-Fi hotspot. The INTERNET permission allows local sockets. The app has no cloud chat account, ads, analytics SDK, subscription or in-app purchase integration. The planned paid download is handled by Google Play; FamilyLink does not receive payment-card details.

What you share

You choose a name and color. Confirmed room members can exchange text, small photos, voice notes, call audio, group alerts and meeting details. The host approves participants, relays messages and can read room content. Recipients may keep copies or screenshots. Names and colors do not prove who is holding a device.

Native Wi-Fi uses pinned TLS certificates; Bluetooth relies on Android pairing and link security. Optional browser guests use unencrypted local HTTP. Network observers may read it and active attackers may alter the page or traffic. Host approval is an admission control, not end-to-end group encryption.

Optional location sharing

A one-time location is shown with age and accuracy for confirmation before sending. Separately, users may enable 30- or 60-minute location sharing, with updates about every five minutes to the host and approved native-app users, including later joiners. Browser guests are excluded from timed updates. Sharing can continue with the screen locked, pauses while disconnected, and stops on expiry, leaving or app restart. Stop controls are available in the app and notification. No ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission is declared; user-started foreground-service location is used.

Timed positions are not stored as movement history. Recipients may retain earlier positions until expiry or keep their own copies. A one-time location sent as a message can remain in saved chat. Opening a Maps link passes coordinates to the chosen external map app or website under its own policy.

Local storage and deletion

Recent chat and reconnection information are stored in private, Android Keystore-backed AES-256-GCM storage. Limits are 80 entries, 10 photos, 10 voice notes and 10 queued messages. Starting or joining another room replaces that local conversation. The app does not promise unlimited retention. Saved data is accessible to someone who can unlock the phone and open the app.

While disconnected, use Saved conversation → Delete Saved Messages and Reconnection Key. Deletion affects this device, not other participants’ copies. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes local data; no cloud restore is provided. Android app-data backup is disabled.

Permissions

Nearby devices and Bluetooth permissions support local discovery, pairing and connections. Nearby Wi-Fi and network permissions support the hotspot. Older Android discovery may require location permission. Camera access supports QR scanning and flashlight signaling; photo selection uses Android’s picker. Microphone access supports user-started voice calls and recordings. Voice-call audio is not intentionally saved as a recording by the app; voice notes are stored when sent. Unsent note drafts are discarded when leaving the app.

Notifications show active chat and sharing status and incoming room alerts. Foreground-service types are connectedDevice, microphone and location, used for their respective active tasks. Location sharing and voice use are optional. The app does not request contacts, phone-call management, SMS or advertising-ID permission.

Feedback and installer sharing

Feedback opens your email app with a draft to tripperdeelabs@gmail.com; you decide whether to send. Optional diagnostics can be previewed and include only app version, Android API, layout, text size, theme, connection type and room state. They exclude names, messages, device identifiers, PINs, locations, network addresses and logs. Your own text and email address can still contain personal information. Contact support to request deletion of correspondence.

Installer sharing exposes only a copied app APK through a temporary read grant. It does not export chat history. Split-package installations cannot be shared as one complete installer. Review links remain hidden until a verified store listing is configured.

Children and launch status

The intended audience is families including children. This is a development preview; required parent controls and child-audience privacy requirements are being finalized before public launch. There is no open public chat directory or stranger-matching feature. Do not treat room PINs or host approval as proof of parental consent.

This website and contact

Website hosting and optional analytics are separate from the app. See the website privacy policy. Questions or requests: tripperdeelabs@gmail.com.
