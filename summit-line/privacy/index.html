Summit Line Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Tripper Dee Labs provides Summit Line, a mountain-inspired Wear OS watch face. This policy describes the Summit Line watch face, package com.tripperdeelabs.summitline.watchface.

Information displayed on your watch

Summit Line uses Wear OS Watch Face Format to display the time, date, battery level, and available steps, heart-rate, weather, and complication information. Availability depends on your device, its settings, and the providers you choose. The daily ring compares native watch steps with a goal selected in the face; it does not store step history or synchronize health-app goals. Automatic scenery changes at fixed local clock times without location data. The moon illustration follows phase information supplied by Wear OS, without a network request. The face does not run its own workout recorder, location tracker, or measurement service.

Collection and sharing

The watch face does not collect or transmit personal information to Tripper Dee Labs. It has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, account system, or app-owned network service. We do not receive the health readings or complication values displayed by the face.

Personal photos

On supported Wear OS 6 setups, personal-photo customization is handled through the watch platform and its supported photo-selection flow. Summit Line displays the selected photo and does not upload it to Tripper Dee Labs. Your watch, paired-phone software, and any photo service you use may process the photo under their own policies.

Other services

Google Play, Wear OS, your device manufacturer, and installed complication providers operate independently and may process information under their own privacy policies. A tap on a supported field may open a system app or provider. Purchasing through Google Play is subject to Google's payment and privacy practices; Summit Line does not receive your payment-card details.

Support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message, and use it to respond and resolve your request. Please avoid sending sensitive health data. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Your choices

You can change or remove complications and personal photos through supported watch settings, manage permissions in the platform/provider settings, or uninstall the face. Summit Line has no account to delete.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the product or its practices change and will update the effective date above. For questions, contact feedback@tripperdeelabs.com.
