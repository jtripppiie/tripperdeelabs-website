Amelia Navigator: Pilot Face · Wear OS

Aviation watch face for Wear OS

Amelia Navigator is an aviation-style watch face for Wear OS. A navy dial, brass details, compass-style markings, and digital time draw on historical flight instruments. It is for people who enjoy that visual style and want everyday watch readings alongside an aircraft animation, without installing a separate phone interface.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

A flight-inspired dial for everyday timekeeping

The round layout combines large digital time with day, date, and seconds. Select a 12-hour or 24-hour display to match how you read time. Navigator Navy and Night Instrument dial styles provide different looks, with Brushed Brass or Monochrome accents around the ring. The quieter ambient display pares the face back for the watch’s always-on mode.

Watch the aircraft approach

At the top of each hour, a twin-engine aircraft approaches and settles beneath the north marker. Tap the face to replay the approach when you want to see it again. The animation is a visual detail of the face, not a live aircraft tracker. Its flight-instrument styling does not turn the decorative ring into a magnetic compass or navigation system.

Use the readings your watch supplies

Battery, steps, heart rate, and weather information come from Wear OS and its providers. What appears depends on the device, sensors, permissions, and configured services. The face does not send those readings to TripperDeeLabs. There is no account, advertising, or analytics in Amelia Navigator. A provider used by the watch can have its own network requirements and privacy practices, especially for fresh weather information.

Frequently asked questions

What operating system does it need?

Amelia Navigator requires Wear OS 5 or newer. This listing is for the watch face, not a phone app or a face for Garmin or Apple Watch.

How do I change its appearance?

Touch and hold the watch face to open the Wear OS picker and customization controls. Choose a dial style and ring accent, then use the supported time-format options.

Is the compass ring a navigation tool?

No. The compass-style ring is decorative. Do not use the face to determine a bearing, plan a route, or replace a navigation instrument.

Why are some health or weather readings missing?

The face displays available Wear OS data. Check the watch sensors, provider settings, and permissions; the face itself does not supply a separate health or weather service.
