Website hosting

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Requests necessarily reach the hosting provider with connection information such as your IP address. GitHub states that Pages visitor IP addresses are logged for security, even without a GitHub login. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Website measurement

Pages with website analytics load Google Analytics automatically when JavaScript is enabled. This site no longer displays an analytics consent banner or uses a stored consent choice. JavaScript-disabled browsing does not load these services. Browser privacy settings and content blockers may block measurement.

Google Analytics

We load Google's analytics script to understand website usage. It can process page visits, browser/device information, referral information and analytics identifiers in cookies. We configure Google signals and advertising personalization signals off. Google receives the network requests; see Google's privacy policy. This is website measurement, not analytics inside Private Eyes.

Homepage visitor counter

When you visit a page displaying the visitor counter, your browser requests an increment from api.counterapi.dev for the shared site counter. The request includes the counter address and a cache-busting timestamp. We omit browser credentials and the referrer header. As with any network request, the provider receives connection information such as your IP address and browser headers. The displayed number counts visits, not unique people.

Contact and feedback

Contact links open your email app. The feedback page can prepare the product name and browser user-agent string, which may contain browser and operating-system details. Review or delete these before sending. Messages go to tripperdeelabs@gmail.com through your email provider and ours, and are used to respond to your request. Do not send private app files, vault patterns or phone credentials.

Retention and choices

You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser settings or block analytics with browser privacy tools. Clearing cookies does not delete records already received by providers. Hosting and analytics providers handle their records under their own policies and service settings; this page does not promise a fixed deletion period for their records. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable obligations.

Questions and updates

TripperDeeLabs publishes this website. Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com with privacy questions. We will update this page when the site's data handling changes.
