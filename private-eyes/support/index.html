Before troubleshooting

Do not uninstall the app, clear its storage, or remove your phone's screen lock to fix a problem with an important vault. Those actions can permanently destroy access. If you can still open the vault, export any irreplaceable files to a secure location first; exported copies are unencrypted.

I cannot unlock

Use the Android device verification prompt, then the vault pattern you created. Your phone PIN or fingerprint cannot replace a forgotten vault pattern. Observe any waiting period shown after incorrect attempts. The developer cannot recover a pattern or device key or remotely unlock files.

A floating button interferes with verification

Current builds ask Android to hide other apps' floating overlays while Private Eyes is visible. The controls should return after leaving. If your phone still blocks interaction, temporarily stop the floating control and reopen Private Eyes. Tell us the phone model, Android version and overlay app name.

Installing or updating

Install updates from the same trusted distribution route. Store Test, Preview and production are separate apps with separate vaults. If Android reports an incompatible signature, keep the existing installation and contact support. Do not uninstall it as a workaround. Vaults do not transfer automatically between these apps.

My original is still visible in Gallery

Importing protects a copy inside the vault. Originals and exported copies remain outside it. Verify the vault copy and keep any needed independent backup before managing originals in the app or provider that holds them.

When does it lock?

Normal app departure, turning off the screen and Lock vault end the session. Brief permission prompts and file selection preserve it; file selection is limited to two minutes. Keeping the app open in the foreground has no inactivity timeout.

Send a useful report

Email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com with the app version, phone model, Android version, steps, expected result and actual result. Use harmless sample files to reproduce issues. Do not send vault contents, patterns, phone PINs or screenshots containing private information. We cannot inspect your vault remotely.
