Untangle Trails support

Clear every crossing

Drag numbered pegs until no cords cross. Gold-ringed fixed pegs stay in place. Any valid clear arrangement wins. The guided tutorial shows your first move.

Hints and progress

Hints reveal one valid solution and affect your star rating. Undo lets you reconsider a move. Avoid clearing app storage or uninstalling while troubleshooting, because local progress can be lost.

Ads, purchases and privacy

Use the settings gear for Remove Ads, purchase restoration and privacy choices when available. Purchases require Google Play connectivity and the account used to buy them. The game is not yet publicly available on Google Play.

Contact

Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com with your game version, phone model and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment details.
