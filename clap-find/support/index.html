ClapFind · Android · In development

Help and support

Get help finding your nearby phone.

ClapFind is still in development. It is not available for download from Google Play yet.

Turn listening on and off

Open ClapFind and tap Start listening before putting your phone down. Android shows an ongoing notification while the microphone service is active. Tap Stop in that notification, or return to the app and turn listening off.

Claps are missed or trigger by mistake

Keep the microphone uncovered and clap within hearing distance. Try High sensitivity in a quiet room or Low in a noisy room. Sudden sounds, distance, phone placement, and device-specific microphone behavior can affect detection.

No sound, vibration, or flashlight

Check your alarm volume and Do Not Disturb settings. Vibration and flashlight alerts depend on the phone’s hardware and Android settings. Microphone access is required for listening; camera access is optional and only used for the flashlight.

Contact

For questions or corrections, email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com. Include your phone model and Android version. Do not send recordings, passwords, or payment details.
