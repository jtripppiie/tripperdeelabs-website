JUST REMIND ME · SUPPORT

A little help,
when you need it.

Start a request

Tap the microphone and allow microphone access when Android asks. Use a clear time such as “tomorrow at 9 AM.” Review the confirmation before continuing. If a request cannot be understood, nothing is created; repeat it with a clear task, date and time.

Add the widget

Tap Add home screen widget and finish your launcher's placement prompt. You can also long-press the home screen and look under Widgets for Just Remind Me. The widget opens the app's voice input.

Speech language unavailable

Android error 13 means the requested recognition language is currently unavailable. Check your speech provider's language/download settings and network connection. Enable a compatible recognition service. On-device service availability does not guarantee that the needed language model is installed. If speech fails, no reminder is scheduled.

Reminder did not arrive

Open the Settings gear in the app for notification and precise-alarm settings. Open Upcoming and tap an entry to cancel it, including future weekly occurrences. Check Android Settings → Apps → Just Remind Me → Notifications. Check Alarms & reminders special access where your device provides it. Menu names vary. Battery restrictions and manufacturer settings may delay alerts. Test a short reminder on your phone before relying on it. Overdue one-time reminders are rescheduled shortly after reopening or reboot, when notification access allows delivery; missed weekly occurrences advance to the next future week.

Assistant button

Open the settings gear → Assistant button. Read the compatibility note and choose the app through Android. On supported phones, configure the side button or gesture for Digital assistant in your phone settings. Options vary by manufacturer and Android version; this is not a guarantee for every model and does not add a wake word. Restore your previous assistant in Android’s default-app settings at any time.

Alarms and calendar events

Use Open alarms and Open calendar on the main screen to check your saved items. Finish saving in the Clock or Calendar app that opens. Check the requested date carefully: Android's alarm intent does not guarantee a specific calendar date. Recurrence is currently implemented only for local reminder notifications, not external Clock/Calendar requests.

Timers and languages

Dedicated countdown timers and conversational follow-ups are not yet implemented. Some localized phrases are normalized, but broad multilingual reliability has not been established. Clear English commands with explicit AM/PM times are the best-tested starting point.

Availability and price

The app is in development, with a planned $0.99 US paid download and no ads or subscription. There is no public Play download link yet. Pricing and countries will be confirmed when the listing is available.

Contact

Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com with your app version, Android version, phone model and a non-sensitive description of what happened. Never send passwords or payment details.
