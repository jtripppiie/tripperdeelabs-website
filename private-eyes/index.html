Private Eyes: Photo Vault Lock

Offline Encrypted Photo Vault for Android

Private Eyes stores selected photos and files in an encrypted vault on your Android phone. Its camera can capture photos and short silent videos directly into the vault.

The app works offline and has no internet permission. Imported originals remain outside the vault until you manage them separately. There is no recovery service or cloud backup, so a lost pattern or device can mean permanent loss.

Private files, kept on your phone

No account or Internet permission

Your vault works offline. The Android app has no analytics, advertising or cloud service.

Protect the file and its details

AES-256-GCM protects file contents, filenames and stored thumbnails. Android device authentication and your pattern control access.

Capture without a Gallery original

Take photos and short silent videos directly into the vault. Imports preserve original quality; export is an explicit action.

From import to locked vault

  1. Unlock and import selected photos, videos or documents.
  2. Review successful imports and retry unfinished files.
  3. Optionally choose Remove originals… and review the source provider or Android confirmation.
  4. Lock the vault. Originals and exports elsewhere remain outside its protection.

Know the limits before you start

Android 13 or newer is required. Imports support up to 1 GiB per file. Camera video is silent and limited to two minutes / 120 MiB, subject to available memory. RAW originals can be stored; preview support varies.

There is no backup, recovery service or pattern reset. Losing the phone, its keys or your pattern can mean permanent loss. Do not keep the only copy of irreplaceable files here. Private Eyes is not independently security-certified and does not promise forensic invisibility.

Common questions

Does importing delete the original?

No. Removal is optional after a successful import and depends on the source provider. Other copies and backups may remain.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. The app works offline and has no sign-up or Internet permission.

Can I recover files on a replacement phone?

There is currently no portable encrypted backup or recovery service. Read the recovery guide before relying on local-only storage.

Are exports encrypted?

Exported copies are readable outside the vault. Choose their destination carefully.
