Glowglass privacy policy

Updated November 25, 2026. This policy covers Glowglass: Light Puzzles, Android version 1.0.1, supplied by TripperDeeLabs. The release candidate uses package com.tripperdeelabs.glowglass; local test builds use com.tripperdeelabs.glowglass.test.

On-device game data

Puzzle progress, unfinished boards, personal best move counts, last-played puzzle, sound and animation settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. TripperDeeLabs does not receive these records and does not operate a gameplay server. There is no account or sign-in.

No advertising or analytics

The game includes no advertising, analytics, consent-management or in-app billing SDK. It requests no Android permissions, including internet access. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising identifier. Puzzles work offline. There is no chat, sharing, user-generated content or online leaderboard.

Purchase and store services

The planned product is a one-time paid download through Google Play, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Google Play handles payment and its own account and store records under Google’s policies. The game does not receive payment-card details. A public store release has not yet occurred.

Privacy and support links

Choosing Privacy policy opens the website in your browser. Choosing Email support opens your email app with a draft; nothing is sent automatically. These other apps and the website may use a connection. If you send an email, we receive the address and information you choose to provide and use them to respond. Please do not send payment details or passwords. Website practices are described separately in the website privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Game records remain on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall. Android app backup is disabled; manufacturer migration behavior can vary. We cannot recover deleted puzzle progress. Buying or reinstalling the app does not restore local progress. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact us. Google handles requests about information Google holds.

Children and contact

The game is designed for people who enjoy thoughtful spatial puzzles. It does not request age or date of birth, and has no child-specific data collection. A parent or guardian may contact us on a child’s behalf. Contact tripperdeelabs@gmail.com for support or privacy questions.
