Wear OS watch faces

These current Google Play releases are for compatible Wear OS watches. They do not run on Garmin watches.

Amelia Navigator: Pilot Face

Amelia Navigator combines a navy aviation-style dial, brass details and digital time.

Dive Watch: Classic & Lume

Dive Watch pairs an analog dial with luminous markers, a date window and a battery indicator inside the left marker.

Kenai Fishing Report Face

Kenai Fishing Report shows time alongside salmon counts, river temperature and tide information on a compatible Wear OS watch.

Launch Vector Watch Face

Launch Vector displays the next rocket launch countdown with time, weather and watch data.

Summit Line

Summit Line places digital time over mountain scenery.
