Wonder Cascade Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Updated November 24, 2026

This policy covers Wonder Cascade (also titled Wonder Cascade: Match 3), including Android version 0.23.0 with Google Mobile Ads 25.5.0, User Messaging Platform 4.0.0, and Google Play Billing 9.1.0. There is no game account or sign-in.

Your puzzles and progress

Journey progress, daily puzzle records, weekly keepsakes, settings, and saved boards are stored on your device. TripperDeeLabs does not receive these records or operate a gameplay server. Clearing app data removes local progress. Puzzles work offline; ads, purchases, and purchase restoration need a connection.

Browser feedback preview

The temporary browser preview at tripperdeelabs.com/wonder-cascade/play/ has no advertising SDK, purchases or analytics. Game files are cached in your browser, and progress and preferences are stored locally using browser storage. Clearing site data removes these saves; private browsing or blocked storage may prevent them from being retained. Browser progress is separate from Android progress.

Our website host receives ordinary connection information when delivering the preview. The Send feedback form keeps your text on the page until you choose to copy it or open an email draft. That draft includes your feedback, preview version, current level and screen, browser identification and display size. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send the email, the support-correspondence practices below apply.

Advertising and younger players

The Android version may show interstitial ads between eligible completed levels. This build applies child-directed and under-age-of-consent tags to all players, requests G-rated ad content, and requests non-personalized ads. It does not ask for date of birth or use an age screen. Advertising-ID and Android advertising-services permissions are removed. These settings do not mean the advertising SDK collects no data.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which can imply approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account-related identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Removing advertising-ID permission does not remove all identifier processing. SDK transmissions use TLS. No separate Firebase Analytics or gameplay analytics integration has been added.

See Google's privacy policy and its Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for information about Google's processing, security and retention.

Privacy choices

A fresh User Messaging Platform (UMP) privacy update and any SDK-required form must finish before ad requests. The app does not choose consent on your behalf. Where required, Settings → Privacy & about → Ad privacy opens the SDK's privacy options. Ads stay unavailable when the privacy check fails, remains unknown, or the SDK disallows requests. The app also checks Google Play purchase ownership and suppresses ads for verified Remove Ads owners.

Remove Ads purchases

Remove Ads is an optional permanent, non-consumable Google Play purchase. There are no paid lives, boosters, or currencies. Google Play processes payment; the app does not receive payment-card details. Signed purchase information is stored locally.

Restore purchases uses the Google Play account on your device. Deleting game data does not cancel the Play purchase; restoring purchases does not restore puzzle progress.

Sharing and feedback

Sharing a daily result happens only after you choose Share and pick another app. A result image and summary are provided to that app; the puzzle solution is not included. Feedback opens an email draft; nothing is sent automatically.

Storage, security and deletion

On Android, saves, recovery copies, preferences and cached purchase receipts use the app's private storage. Android app backup is disabled. Google SDKs may also store privacy and service information locally. Local app data remains until it is removed through app or operating-system controls.

To delete local data, open Android Settings, select Wonder Cascade under Apps and clear storage, or uninstall the game. Menu names vary by device. This resets game progress; we cannot restore it from a server. Deleting the app does not delete records independently held by Google. Use Google's privacy and account controls or contact Google concerning those records. Google's SDK disclosures describe encryption in transit; no storage or transmission method is guaranteed risk-free.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that correspondence to respond, investigate issues and follow up. Your email provider and ours process correspondence for delivery and storage. The game does not automatically attach your saved puzzle, purchase receipt or device files.

We retain support correspondence as needed to address your request, related follow-up or applicable obligations. You or a parent or guardian can request deletion of correspondence by emailing tripperdeelabs@gmail.com. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support. Delete local progress using device controls; Google handles requests about information it holds.

Website and external services

Opening this policy, an email app or another external service is separate from playing a puzzle. Our website host receives connection information when serving this page; see our website privacy policy. External services have their own privacy practices.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, support or requests concerning support correspondence, contact TripperDeeLabs at tripperdeelabs@gmail.com. Changes will be posted here with an updated date and applicable app version.
