Tower Breadcrumbs support

The app is in development for Android 10 and later. Contact tripperdeelabs@gmail.com.

Before heading out

  1. Import a legally obtained regional raster MBTiles map in Offline. PNG, JPEG and WebP tiles are supported, up to 500 MB. Vector PBF tiles are not supported.
  2. Optionally import cell locations as CSV with latitude,longitude,id,carrier,radio,source headers. OpenCellID-style lat/lon files are also supported, up to 20,000 rows. Carrier names and radio types must come from the source; check its license and data age.
  3. Go outdoors, choose Record a trail and allow precise location. Optional phone-state access supplies cellular details.
  4. Check that coordinates and the point count update before you depend on the recorded trail.

Returning to reception

Select Last reception or Last data on Map. The first is a recorded service indication; the second is an Android-validated cellular connection. The compass is north-up and displays a true bearing, not your phone’s magnetic heading. Distance is straight-line only. Check terrain yourself.

Understanding tower labels

Labels show the imported carrier and radio type. NR maps to 5G, LTE to 4G, UMTS/WCDMA/HSPA to 3G, and GSM/EDGE/GPRS to 2G. Numeric network codes remain PLMN codes unless the file provides a carrier name. An estimated cell location is not a surveyed tower or coverage guarantee.

GPS or reception appears unavailable

Check Android Location and precise-location permission. Move outdoors and give GPS time to acquire satellites. Stale or low-accuracy positions are not used for return bearings. Cell readings depend on your hardware, permissions, SIM and Android restrictions. A Wi-Fi connection is not recorded as cellular internet. Some 5G non-standalone connections may be reported as LTE by Android.

Saving, exporting and stopping

Use Stop GPS & Save to finish recording. GPS navigation also has a Stop control and a notification action. Select a journey in Logbook to review it or export CSV. The map draws the most recent 2,000 points of the selected trail; the export contains all saved points. Avoid uninstalling or clearing storage during troubleshooting.

Ad removal

The planned one-time US price is $0.99. Actual prices come from Google Play after the product is configured. Use Restore purchases with the purchasing Google account. Preview installs may show Purchase unavailable; no charge can be made until the Play product and testing distribution are configured.

Report a problem

Include app version, phone model, Android version, whether a SIM was present, and the steps that caused the issue. You do not need to send exact coordinates or a complete trail.
