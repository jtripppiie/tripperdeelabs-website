Honest expectations

Large cleanups take time.

X loads and processes items individually. Thousands of likes or posts may take hours or multiple sessions. X may temporarily throttle activity, stop returning older items, or change its interface. Post Cleaner works gradually, pauses between actions, and reports what it actually removed—it does not promise that every item can be removed in a single run.

Posts cannot be recovered after deletion. Users receive a clear confirmation before cleanup begins and should start with the one-item test.

Local by design

Cleanup happens inside your browser.

The extension interacts with the X page already open in Firefox. Its trial state, cleanup report, and rolling diagnostic events remain in local extension storage. Feedback is optional and opens the user's email application before anything is sent.
