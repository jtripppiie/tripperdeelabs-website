Tide Window Companion Privacy Policy

Updated November 21, 2026. Publisher: TripperDeeLabs. Contact: tripperdeelabs@gmail.com. This policy covers com.tripperdeelabs.tidewindowcompanion, the networked Wear OS companion. The separate resource-only Tide Window watch face is covered by https://tripperdeelabs.com/privacy/watch-faces/.

What the app does

You choose a public NOAA tide-prediction station. The companion downloads predictions directly from NOAA over HTTPS and supplies them to watch complications. No account, GPS location, continuous location tracking, contacts, microphone or health data is requested.

Information sent to NOAA

Requests contain the selected station ID, requested prediction dates and a generic application identifier shared by all installations. NOAA also receives ordinary network information, including your IP address and request timing. The selected station is not necessarily your physical location. We do not operate a server that receives these requests. NOAA describes its own information handling at https://www.noaa.gov/protecting-your-privacy. We cannot control or delete NOAA server logs.

Information on your watch

The selected station name and ID, downloaded predictions and fetch timestamp are saved in app preferences. Preferences are excluded from the app’s cloud and device-transfer backup configuration. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Your controls

Clear saved station removes the stored station and prediction cache and stops future prediction requests until you select a station again. An already in-flight request may finish, but a result for the cleared station is discarded. Android app-storage controls and uninstalling can also remove local app data. Refreshing or using a tide complication after selecting a station can trigger a network request. Fresh cached predictions can be displayed offline; stale data is marked unavailable.

Children

The companion does not ask for an age, birthday or account. Its NOAA network requests are the same for every user. No advertising, analytics or child profiling is included. A parent or guardian can use the local clearing controls or contact us with privacy questions.

Support

If you choose to email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com, we use the information you send to respond. Do not include payment details or sensitive personal information. The app does not automatically send support messages.

Changes

We will post changes to this policy here and update the date above.
