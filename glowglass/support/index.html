Glowglass support

Glowglass: Light Puzzles 1.0.2 is currently a development candidate, not a public Google Play release.

Make your first connection

Choose Begin with light. Tap the mirror once and watch the cyan beam reach its receiver. Every puzzle is complete when all receivers have exactly their labeled color. C means cyan, G gold, V violet and W white. Light updates after every gesture.

Glass pieces

  • Mirrors reflect light through a right angle. Tap to switch between their two angles.
  • Splitters marked + send light straight ahead and along the reflected path.
  • Prisms separate white light into cyan, gold and violet through three labeled ports. Tap to rotate all ports together.
  • Filters pass one color. Tap to cycle cyan, gold and violet.
  • Mirrors marked A turn together. A single tap changes both.
  • Mirrors with three dots can move. Drag one into an empty dashed socket; the beam previews a valid placement before release. An invalid drop keeps the original position.

Hints, undo and restart

A little hint first highlights a piece. Choose it again for a specific step toward one authored solution. Hints are free and never make the move for you. Undo reverses a move, including Restart, during the current puzzle session. Returning Home or reopening a puzzle starts a new undo history. Restart leaves your previous completion record intact.

Chapters and records

Use the arrows beside the chapter name on Home to browse six collections of ten puzzles. All are open. Continue returns to your last unfinished puzzle, or the next unfinished puzzle after a completion. Your best move count and chapter completion remain on this device.

Comfort and privacy

Settings & about includes Sound and Light animation switches, privacy and email links. Disabling light animation removes moving beam sparks; all puzzle rules and controls remain available. Android Back closes a panel, returns from a puzzle to Home, or offers Save & exit.

Keep your progress

Do not clear app storage or uninstall when troubleshooting if you want to retain progress. There is no account or cloud restore. The separate test and production packages have separate saves. A storage warning means progress could not be saved; check free device space. An unreadable save is not overwritten automatically.

Contact

Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com with your version, device model, puzzle number and what happened. Nothing is emailed automatically.
