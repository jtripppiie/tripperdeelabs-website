RoboBall: Arcade Basketball · Android

Offline arcade basketball for Android

RoboBall is an arcade basketball game for Android built around timing your shot. Watch the power meter, choose when to release, and see the ball react to the hoop and its surroundings. It suits players who want short shooting challenges, a place to practice, and progress they can keep on their own phone.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Learn the release, then adjust your aim

Start with untimed practice or guided practice to get a feel for the meter. A successful shot depends on timing and aim, while the ball interacts with the rim, backboard, and obstacles. Once the basic release feels familiar, moving baskets, wind, and changes in gravity give you different conditions to work with. Replaying a court lets you try a different approach.

Explore courts and keep improving

The game moves beyond a single basketball cabinet. Its settings include city stages, rooftops, railways, underwater courts, planetary gravity challenges, and Kenai Salmon Shot. Review your result after a run and replay to earn more stars. Scores, progression, and trophies are saved on the device, making the challenge about your own attempts rather than an online leaderboard.

Set up a comfortable game

Options cover audio, haptics, contrast, the heads-up display, motion, and the aiming guide. Use these controls to make the shooting feedback easier to follow on your phone. The game pauses when interrupted. It runs offline without ads, a subscription, or an account, so playing does not depend on a signal or a sign-in service.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play on a flight without Wi-Fi?

Yes. RoboBall runs offline after installation. Scores, stars, and trophies are saved locally on the phone.

Is there a practice mode?

Yes. Untimed practice and guided practice help you learn the release before you work through timed challenges and changing court conditions.

Does progress transfer through an online account?

No account is required and progression is local. Do not rely on a cloud profile to restore your scores when changing phones or removing app data.

Does the game contain ads or subscriptions?

No. RoboBall is a one-time purchase with no ads or subscriptions. The current price and device availability are shown on Google Play.
