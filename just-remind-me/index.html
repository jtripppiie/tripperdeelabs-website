A SMALL TASK SHOULD STAY SIMPLE

Tap. Speak. Check the details.

  1. Open Just Remind Me or tap its home-screen widget.
  2. Speak a supported command with a clear date and time.
  3. Review the details and confirm. For alarms and calendar events, finish in the app that opens.

“Remind me to call Steve tomorrow at 9 AM.”

Or try “Remind me in 30 minutes to check the oven,” “Wake me up tomorrow at 7 AM,” or “Put a dentist appointment on my calendar next Tuesday at 10 AM.”

Useful, without another subscription.

The planned release is a one-time $0.99 US purchase, with no advertising or paid AI service. Final local prices and availability will appear on Google Play at launch.

Everything you need for a simple reminder.

  • One-time and weekly reminders: use today, tomorrow, a named weekday, or a delay in minutes or hours. Confirm the task and time first.
  • Upcoming reminders: see what is scheduled and cancel a reminder or its future weekly repeats.
  • Clock and Calendar shortcuts: create an alarm or event in your installed apps, then use Open alarms and Open calendar to check it.
  • Quick voice access: tap the microphone, add the home-screen widget, or choose optional assistant-button setup on a supported phone.
  • Optional side-button access: choose Just Remind Me in Android’s digital-assistant settings, then configure your phone’s assistant gesture or button. Availability varies by phone; not every model is supported.
  • Clear setup: Settings shows notification, precise-timing and assistant status, with explanations before opening Android settings.
  • Recovery after restart: saved reminders are restored. Missed one-time reminders are rescheduled; missed weekly occurrences move to the next future week.
  • Simple sharing: share the public app link without exposing your reminders. Optional Google Play reviews become available after launch.

Built around Android, with clear limits.

Speech uses your installed Android recognition service. The system speech screen is preferred when available, with offline recognition requested; the in-app fallback prefers on-device recognition. Language downloads or an internet connection may be needed. Your recognition provider may process audio under its own privacy policy.

Reminder delivery depends on notification permission, alarm access and Android device settings. Countdown timers, texting, reminder editing and conversational follow-up questions are not included in this release. Physical-device reliability testing is ongoing.
