Alaska Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026. Published by TripperDeeLabs.

Offline play

Alaska Rush is an offline game. No account is required. The game contains no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, in-app purchases, or developer-operated server connection. TripperDeeLabs does not receive gameplay data from the app.

Information on your device

The game stores progress, unlocked trails, scores, best times, stars, cave discoveries, runner choice, sound and accessibility settings in its private local storage. It can import existing progress from an older installed version of the same app.

Optional photo

If you choose a local photo, the game reads that selected image to personalize your runner and stores its local reference. Image processing takes place on your device; the game does not upload the photo. You can remove the reference with Reset Photo. Original photos are not deleted.

Permissions and other services

The release game does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or broad photo-library access. Photo selection uses the system picker; availability of a cloud-backed image depends on your chosen document provider. Optional vibration is controlled in Settings. Developer review and speech-note tools are disabled in the store build.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the app storage or uninstall the game. Reset Photo removes the selected image reference. The game does not operate a cloud account or server database to delete. Android, your device manufacturer, and the app store may independently process purchase, delivery, or diagnostic information under their own policies.

Contact and links

If you email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com, we receive the address and information you choose to send. Do not include sensitive personal information. Opening our website or your email app is optional and subject to the policies of those services.

Changes

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change. Questions about this policy can be sent to feedback@tripperdeelabs.com.
