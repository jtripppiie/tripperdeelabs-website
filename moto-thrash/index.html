Moto Thrash: Retro Motocross · Android

Offline motocross racing and track building

Moto Thrash is a retro motocross game for Android with racing, bike balance, and a local course builder. Race computer opponents across 24 tracks in four cups, or step away from the race schedule to design a course. It is for players who enjoy learning jumps and experimenting with an arcade dirt bike.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Balance the bike and manage your boost

Use the on-screen directional pad to change lanes and control bike balance. Keeping the bike level in the air helps you line up a landing. Wheelies and front-wheel balances give you more ways to ride, but pushing too far costs stability. Boost adds another decision: watch engine heat and choose when the extra speed will help you through the track.

Build a course you can ride

The editor provides six local save slots. Place ramps, bumps, mud, oil, boost pads, and jump hazards, then ride the result. Water, rivers, cars, buses, and other scenery or hazards let you try different arrangements. Custom tracks are stored on the device; the editor is a way to experiment on your own phone, without an online publishing or sharing service.

Try a different kind of jump

Stunt Show lets you set up a bus-jumping attempt with a chosen ramp and run-up speed. Work toward a clean landing and compare the result with your personal best. Racing and custom courses also work offline. There are no ads, in-app purchases, or accounts, and progress stays on the device rather than in a developer-operated cloud save.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play Moto Thrash without internet?

Yes. The racing, course builder, and Stunt Show work offline after installation. No account or online session is needed to play.

Can I share tracks or race other people online?

No. Moto Thrash is single-player. You race computer opponents, and the six custom-course slots are local to your device.

How many tracks are included?

The game includes 24 tracks across four cups. The course builder and Stunt Show provide separate ways to experiment with jumps and layouts.

What happens to my custom courses if I clear storage?

Courses and progress are local app data. Clearing storage or uninstalling can remove them; the game does not provide a cloud-save account.
