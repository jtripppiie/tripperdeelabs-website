Survival privacy policy

SURVIVAL — PRIVACY POLICY
Effective November 9, 2026
App: com.tripperdeelabs.survival
Publisher: TripperDeeLabs • tripperdeelabs@gmail.com

Survival is a paid, offline-first reference and planning app. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK or in-app purchases. Google Play handles the upfront app payment under its own privacy practices; Survival does not receive payment-card details.

ON YOUR DEVICE
Guides, downloaded maps, kit/pantry choices, saved waypoints and breadcrumb trails are stored in app storage. The app does not send them to a TripperDeeLabs server. No background-location permission is requested. Location tools operate while their screens are in use; permission can be revoked in Android Settings. Location estimates, compass readings and altitude may be inaccurate or unavailable.

OPTIONAL ONLINE FEATURES
Browsing/downloading regional maps contacts the Mapsforge mirror at Hochschule Esslingen. That service receives your IP address and requested catalog/region. Android DownloadManager may continue a confirmed download after you leave the screen; Wi-Fi is selected by default, roaming is disabled, and the app offers cancellation.
USGS local topographic downloads send tile coordinates identifying your chosen area and your IP address to the USGS map service.
Nearby-place refresh sends coordinates to OpenStreetMap Overpass and, for U.S. coverage, FEMA to obtain nearby records. Find my region sends coordinates to the device's Android Geocoder provider. These optional operations involve precise location/selected area and third-party processing. Their operators control their own logs and retention; this app cannot delete those logs.
Source and credit links open the external browser. Providers may receive normal connection information. No connection is needed to read bundled guides or open already-saved regional maps.

CAMERA / LIGHT
Camera permission is used only to operate the flashlight/SOS torch where hardware supports it. Survival does not record or upload photographs, video or audio.

SHARING AND CALLS
Coordinate sharing opens the Android share sheet only at your request. The recipient/app you choose receives the shared information and applies its own privacy policy. Emergency call controls open the dialer; they do not automatically place calls or contact rescuers.

DELETION / CONTROLS
Delete regional maps in Offline regions, delete saved waypoints or clear the breadcrumb trail in Navigate, and edit kit/pantry entries in the app. Android Settings > Apps > Survival > Storage > Clear storage removes local app records; uninstall also removes app storage. Cancel a running map download before clearing storage/uninstalling. No remote user account exists to delete. Android cloud app backup is disabled; platform/device-transfer behavior may depend on the OS.

CONTACT AND CHANGES
Questions or corrections: tripperdeelabs@gmail.com. Future updates may revise this policy and its effective date. No sale of user data or targeted advertising is implemented by Survival.
