Watch Faces Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Effective November 10, 2026

This policy covers TripperDeeLabs resource-only Wear OS watch faces whose store listing links to this policy and whose features match the practices below. It currently includes Retro Arcade (com.tripperdeelabs.retroarcadewatch). It does not cover the watch-face builder, companion apps, or products with their own policy. Those are listed in our privacy directory.

Watch data stays on your watch

These are resource-only watch faces. Depending on the design, they display time, date, step count, battery level, unread-notification status and information from the complication provider you select, such as heart rate. It does not read or display the contents of your messages.

The watch face has no developer-operated backend, network permission, account system, advertising, analytics or in-app purchases. It does not transmit these readings to TripperDeeLabs, and TripperDeeLabs does not receive or store them on a server.

Complications, permissions and third parties

Wear OS and installed complication providers supply the displayed data. Those providers control measurement frequency, permissions, storage and any sharing under their own privacy policies. Their data practices are separate from this watch face. You can change or remove the complication in your watch's customization menu and manage provider permissions in watch settings.

Heart-rate readings are for general information. These watch faces do not diagnose health conditions or provide medical advice.

Purchases and downloads

Google Play handles purchases, installation and updates under Google's terms and privacy policy. The watch face does not receive payment-card details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Support correspondence

If you email TripperDeeLabs, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive health readings or payment details. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us; information may be retained where required for legal obligations or resolving disputes.

Children

The watch face does not collect personal information from children or other users. Any selected third-party provider is governed by its own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when the watch face's data practices change. This policy concerns the watch face; the website privacy policy explains the studio website's separate practices.

Contact

Developer: TripperDeeLabs. For privacy questions or requests, email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com.
