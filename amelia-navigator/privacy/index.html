Amelia Navigator Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Updated October 7, 2026

Amelia Navigator is a Wear OS watch face. It does not create user accounts, display advertisements, transmit analytics, or send personal information to the developer.

Watch data

The watch face displays time, date, battery level, step count, heart rate, and weather information supplied locally by Wear OS. The application does not transmit or retain those values on developer-operated servers.

Permissions and third parties

The Wear OS implementation uses system Watch Face Format data sources. Device and platform providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

Children

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other user.

Google Play

Google Play handles purchases and downloads under Google's own policies. TripperDeeLabs does not receive your payment-card information from the watch face.

Changes

If the application's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the affected version is released.

Contact

Developer: TripperDeeLabs. For privacy questions, email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com.
