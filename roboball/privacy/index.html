RoboBall

Privacy Policy

Effective July 31, 2026

The short version: RoboBall is an offline game with no advertising, analytics, account, purchases, or tracking. Player information and progress stay on the device.

Who publishes RoboBall

RoboBall: Worlds of Arcade Basketball is published by TripperDeeLabs. Privacy questions may be sent to TripperDeeLabs support.

Information handled by the game

RoboBall does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The player name, settings, progress, local high scores, and accessibility choices entered in the game are stored only on the player's device. They are not uploaded to TripperDeeLabs or any third party.

RoboBall does not include advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud saves, remote leaderboards, location tracking, or in-app purchases. The game does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, or device identifiers. Optional vibration is used only to provide gameplay haptic feedback.

Children

RoboBall is designed as a family-friendly arcade game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other player. A player should not use their full legal name as their local scoreboard name.

Data retention and deletion

All game data remains on the device. Players can reset progress and scores from Settings. Uninstalling the game removes its local data. RoboBall disables Android application-data backup in its release configuration.

External links

The About screen can open the TripperDeeLabs website in the device browser. Visiting the website is outside the game. The website does not use advertising or behavioral analytics, but external hosting and email providers process requests under their own terms.

Changes to this policy

If RoboBall's data practices materially change, this page and its effective date will be updated before the corresponding app release.

Contact

Privacy questions can be emailed to TripperDeeLabs support.
