Captain Quack: Space Shooter · Android

Offline arcade space shooter for Android

Captain Quack is an arcade space shooter starring a duck in a spaceship. Steer through alien formations, avoid asteroids, and keep firing as each run gets harder. The game is for players looking for a straightforward touch-controlled arcade challenge, with scores and career ranks saved on their own Android device.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Steer, shoot, and watch the whole screen

A touch joystick controls movement, and a separate fire button handles shooting. You need to make room for your ship while aiming at the enemies ahead. Alien formations, asteroids, and gravity wells change what you need to respond to. Clearing an entire formation earns a bonus, so choosing where to move can matter as much as holding the fire button.

Use pickups during a run

Shield, rapid-fire, wide-shot, and extra-life pickups can help you keep a run going. Their value depends on the situation you are facing: a different weapon or a little protection may change how you approach a crowded screen. Pause when you need a break, and use the separate music and sound controls to suit where you are playing.

Keep a personal score to beat

The game records a local top five and career ranks. These give you a record to return to without requiring an account or an online leaderboard. Captain Quack runs offline after installation and contains no ads or in-app purchases. Scores and settings remain on the device. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes that local progress, so it is worth knowing where your records live.

Frequently asked questions

Does Captain Quack need Wi-Fi?

No. The game works offline after installation. You do not need an account or a connection to play a run and save a score.

How do I control the ship?

Use the touch joystick to steer and the dedicated fire button to shoot. Watch for pickups while avoiding asteroids, alien formations, and gravity wells.

Can I pause and change the sound?

Yes. Pause and resume your run, and use the music and sound settings when you want a quieter session.

Are scores stored online?

No. The top-five scores and career ranks stay on your device. Removing app data or uninstalling removes local progress; there is no account-based score recovery.
