Stick Sprint privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026. Developer: TripperDeeLabs.

This policy covers the current Android version of Stick Sprint.

Offline gameplay and local storage

Stick Sprint works offline. The game does not collect or transmit personal information to TripperDeeLabs, and includes no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. It does not require an account and has no in-app purchases.

Scores, coins, unlocked styles, reward claims and preferences are stored in the app’s local storage. There is no game-operated cloud backup or sync service. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can erase progress.

Sharing and external links

Sharing is optional. The game opens Android’s share chooser with game information, and may include your score and selected run theme. You choose whether and where to send it. The receiving app handles the shared text under its own policy. Website and future store links open external services under their own policies.

Support correspondence

If you email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use this correspondence to respond and resolve support requests, retain it as needed for that purpose, and do not sell it. You can request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to any necessary legal retention.

Children and permissions

The game has no chat, user profiles or personal-information submission form. It does not request access to location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Website privacy and updates

This app policy is separate from the website privacy policy, which covers this site and its optional analytics. If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.
