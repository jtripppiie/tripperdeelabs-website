Remove original photos after importing into a vault

Check successful imports, review remaining copies and understand Android’s source deletion confirmation.

Reviewed October 26, 2026 · TripperDeeLabs

Importing a photo into Private Eyes creates an encrypted vault copy. The original can still be visible in Gallery or Files. Remove that source only after you have checked the import and decided how you will preserve anything irreplaceable.

Use the import result

  1. Unlock Private Eyes and choose Import. Select the photos, videos or documents you want.
  2. Read the result: imported, failed and not processed files are reported separately. Retry unfinished files when needed.
  3. Open important vault copies to check that they are usable. Remember that an unsupported preview does not necessarily mean the original failed to import.
  4. At the import result, choose Remove originals… if you want to remove the successfully imported sources. Review Android’s confirmation before accepting.

Only successful imports are offered for removal. Some file providers cannot grant deletion access or support bulk removal. In those cases, Private Eyes explains that you need to manage the source in Gallery or Files. Choosing Done leaves originals in place.

A source is not every copy

A photo may also exist in a cloud library, a message attachment, another device, a download or a trash folder. Private Eyes cannot enumerate or erase all those copies. Deleting a local source does not prove that a cloud copy has disappeared.

Google Photos distinguishes deleting an item from removing it from a device, and deletion can affect other devices with backup enabled. Review Google’s current deletion guidance before changing a synced library. Do not treat cloud deletion as interchangeable with local cleanup.

Keep the tradeoff visible

Private Eyes currently has no encrypted backup or recovery service. If you remove the only other copy and later lose this phone, its protected keys or your pattern, your file may be permanently lost. Keep irreplaceable material somewhere you trust, understanding that exported copies are outside vault protection.

Deletion also does not guarantee physical erasure from flash storage. For future captures, the private camera avoids creating a Gallery original in the first place.
