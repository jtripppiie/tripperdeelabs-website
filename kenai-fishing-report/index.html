Kenai Fishing Report Face · Wear OS

Kenai fishing reports on your Wear OS watch

Kenai Fishing Report is a Wear OS watch face that places public fishing-report information beside the time. With the separate Kenai Report Data companion installed, it can show a sockeye count, river temperature, and the next predicted tide. It is designed for people who follow Kenai Peninsula reports and want a quick view on their watch.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Install the face and its data companion

Install Kenai Report Data on the same watch, then select Kenai Fishing Report as the watch face. Assign the Kenai complication providers if they are not selected automatically. The companion retrieves the reports; the face arranges them on the dial. You do not need a phone app or studio account, but the watch needs internet access for fresh information.

Understand what each reading represents

The fish field shows the latest reported Kenai late-run sockeye count and season total from ADF&G. The water temperature is for the USGS Soldotna station. The tide field uses NOAA Cape Kasilof predictions in Alaska time. Tap for details and source links, and read the count date and stale-report label. A predicted coastal tide is not a measured Kenai River level.

Make the display yours

Choose river scenery or a black backdrop, then select an accent color and time format. The face includes date, battery, and moon phase, with a minimal always-on view. You can use native steps and heart-rate readings in place of the river and tide row. Available readings depend on the watch and providers. Neither the face nor companion requires an account or includes developer-operated tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need Kenai Report Data?

It supplies the fishing-report complications. Install it separately on the same watch; the watch face alone does not download the river, fish, and tide reports.

How current are the reports?

Updates are requested about every 30 minutes, but Wear OS can defer them and sources can be delayed. Older data is marked stale; off-season counts may remain unchanged for a long time.

Does a fish count mean fishing is open?

No. Reports are unofficial context, not permission to fish or a catch forecast. Check current official regulations, emergency orders, and local conditions.

Which devices are supported?

Wear OS 5 or newer with Watch Face Format 2 is required. The face is for Wear OS watches, not phones, Garmin, or Apple Watch.
