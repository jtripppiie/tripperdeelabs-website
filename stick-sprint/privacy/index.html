Math Sprint privacy policy

Updated November 20, 2026. Developer: TripperDeeLabs.

This policy covers Math Sprint (formerly Stick Sprint), Android package com.tripperdeelabs.mathsprint. It does not cover the separate Math Sprint Castle game.

Offline gameplay and local records

Math Sprint gameplay works offline. There are no paid advertisements, game accounts, chat, or developer-operated analytics. Scores, coins, settings, topic progress, question attempts and foreground screen-time summaries are stored privately on the device. Uninstalling or clearing storage can erase those records; there is no developer-operated cloud backup service.

Google Play purchases

The optional Full Math pack uses Google Play for purchase and restore. Google processes payments under its own terms and privacy practices. TripperDeeLabs does not receive payment-card details. The game stores Google's signed purchase payload and signature privately on the device to validate access offline, and reconciles purchase status through Google Play when connected. No purchase tokens or learning records are sent to a developer server. Refund or revocation changes take effect on the next successful Play reconciliation; they cannot be detected while fully offline.

Google Play and its billing services may process purchase, device and diagnostic information under Google's privacy policy. Deleting local game data or contacting TripperDeeLabs does not delete Google's transaction records. Payment and purchase-history requests must also be directed to Google where applicable.

Reports, sharing and external links

Parents can generate PDF and CSV learning reports locally and choose a destination through Android. No report is sent automatically. Sharing a score or game invitation is also optional. You choose the receiving app, which handles shared information under its own policies. Optional external links open the selected external service.

Children and permissions

The app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising-ID permission. It includes Internet, network-state and Google Play Billing permissions for purchase services; gameplay does not require a connection. There is no public profile or personal-information submission form in the game. The deliberate parent hold is not proof of adult identity. Purchase authentication and any family approvals are handled by Google Play.

Support and deletion

If you email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use support correspondence to respond and resolve requests, retain it as necessary for those purposes, and do not sell it. Please avoid sending sensitive information about a child. Request deletion of support correspondence at the same address, subject to required legal retention.

Game records are held on your device rather than a TripperDeeLabs server. You can remove them through Android's app-storage controls or by uninstalling; this erases local progress and cannot be undone through a studio account.

Website and policy changes

This app policy is separate from the website privacy policy, which covers this site's optional analytics. Changes to the game's data practices will be reflected here.
