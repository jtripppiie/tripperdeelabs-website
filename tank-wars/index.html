Tank Wars

Aim high. Change the ground.

Line up your angle, choose your power and send a shell across the hills. Every crater changes the battlefield—and your next shot.

See the battlefield · How to play

In development. Not yet available on Google Play.

Make every shot count

Tank Wars is a native Android, turn-based artillery game. Adjust your turret angle and firing power, watch the wind, then fire. Projectiles follow curved trajectories, explosions carve away terrain, and tanks fall when the ground underneath them disappears.

Three ways to play

Practice against the computer, trade turns with a friend on one phone or tablet, or try the paired Bluetooth Host a game / Join a game mode. Local gameplay needs no internet and no account.

Bluetooth is implemented in the development build, with the host calculating shots, damage and turns. Synchronization is tested in software; two-device radio gameplay still requires validation. Both devices need Bluetooth enabled, Android pairing and the same app version.

A different battlefield

Battle across rolling hills, ridgelines, trenches, border walls and fortified outposts. Tanks start farther apart on flat firing positions. Concrete defenses block shells and break down under explosions, so the battlefield changes with every hit.

Choose your weapon

Shell gives you unlimited standard shots. Heavy makes a wider blast, with three rounds per battle. Drill digs deeper into the ground, also with three rounds. The illustrated weapon chooser shows your equipped shell and remaining ammunition. Use terrain damage to change your opponent’s position, but protect your own foothold.

Inside the game

Actual Android development-build captures from the emulator. Final release details may change.

Play at your pace

Pause mid-shot, then resume where you left off. Local battles save on your device. Sound is optional. Compact controls keep the battlefield visible on phones and tablets.

Between battles

Development builds use Google test interstitial ads only after a completed battle, when starting another battle or returning Home. Close the ad to continue. If no ad is ready, play continues normally. Ads require internet; gameplay does not. Launch pricing and production ad configuration have not been announced.
