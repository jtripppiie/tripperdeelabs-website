Tank Wars support

Aim and fire

Play in landscape. Move the Aim slider to change your turret angle and the Power slider to change shot strength. Tap the weapon name to choose Shell, Heavy or Drill. Tap FIRE when it is your turn. Drag across the battlefield to aim, or use the sliders for finer adjustments. A short dotted guide shows the initial wind-aware trajectory; each tank remembers its previous angle and power. Wind bends the trajectory. The turn passes after the shot and any falling tanks have settled.

Win the battle

Reduce the other tank’s health to zero. Nearby explosions damage tanks; falling can cause additional damage. Shell is unlimited, while Heavy and Drill each start with three rounds. Heavy creates a wider blast; Drill digs deeper. Two players takes turns on the same device; Play computer gives you a computer opponent.

Pause and resume

The two-bar icon pauses. Resume continues a shot or settling terrain. Home keeps the local battle, including ammunition and terrain. Starting a different local battle asks before replacing it. The gear opens Settings; the Home icon returns to the menu. Do not clear storage or uninstall to troubleshoot if you want to keep your save.

Bluetooth setup

Enable Bluetooth and pair both devices in Android Bluetooth settings. Install the same Tank Wars version on both. Choose Bluetooth play, then Host a game on one phone and Join a game on the other. Grant Nearby devices permission and select the hosting phone. The host controls the simulation; each player can aim and fire only on their own turn. Leaving the foreground disconnects the session. Reconnect to start a new battle. Two-device radio behavior is still being validated.

Ads

Players under 18 or with unknown age play without ads. Adults can use Settings → Ad privacy to review available choices or retry a failed privacy check. When approved and enabled, ads are non-personalized and appear only after a completed battle, on New battle or Home. Close the ad to continue; no ready ad means continue immediately. This signed candidate keeps production advertising disabled. Review builds use demo ads and still require privacy setup. Bluetooth sessions close before advertising; host and join again for another battle.
