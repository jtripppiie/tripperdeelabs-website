Math Sprint Castle: math meets an endless runner

Math comes first. Running makes it fun. Math Sprint Castle pairs short math puzzles with a colorful endless run across a winding castle bridge. Choose your runner, collect coins, and learn when to jump, duck or change lanes.

Solve, earn time, then run

The current development build gives five free running minutes a day. Correct answers earn additional time, with short celebrations for streaks and milestones. Questions and running happen separately, so children can concentrate on thinking before returning to the course. Unused time is saved when a run ends early.

A course that asks you to pay attention

Knights lower their lances, wizards aim spells, dragons target a lane with fire, and wind moves across the bridge. Jump hurdles and broken sections, duck under barriers, or find an open lane. Speed and obstacle density rise as a run continues; more complex patterns also depend on demonstrated progress. There is no finish line to memorize.

Watch the computer, then try your own approach

Watch computer is a separate practice preview with pause, restart, course-difficulty previews and a Show math control. It plays the actual course and explains its decisions. During math breaks the road waits while the computer calculates an answer and shows the explanation. The preview does not spend or earn your saved play time and coins.

Choose a runner and make progress

Twelve named runners appear together, with different hair, clothing and sneaker colors. Character choices are cosmetic and have equal abilities. Collect earned coins for in-game unlocks, build your math streaks, and try to improve your own score. The current game saves progress locally and runs offline.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download Math Sprint Castle from Google Play?

Not yet. Math Sprint Castle is in development and undergoing local and device testing. This page is a project preview, not a public release or a download offer.

Does the computer mode complete my progress for me?

No. It is a watchable practice preview with separate time and coins. It does not solve questions in your saved game or unlock your personal rewards.

Are math questions shown while I dodge obstacles?

No. Math and running are separate activities. In the preview, the course pauses during the explanation so the player can focus on the question.

Can I buy math packs now?

The development build includes earned-coin unlocks and a preview of a future All Access option. It does not currently process real-money payments. Final public availability will be stated here when ready.
