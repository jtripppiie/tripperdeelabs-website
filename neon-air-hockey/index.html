Two players. One screen.

Set a phone or tablet between you. Each player controls a mallet on their half, with both fingers moving at once.

Keep the action moving

Pause with one tap. Reset a stuck puck without losing the score. Resume your saved local match when you return.

Bluetooth play is in testing

The development build includes Host a game and Join a game for paired phones. One phone runs the match while the other sends its mallet movements. Two-phone gameplay and connection quality are still being verified, so Bluetooth is not yet a verified release feature.

Simple play, clear limits

Local gameplay works offline, with optional puck sounds and no account. Full-screen ads may appear only between completed matches, when you choose a rematch or return Home. Close the ad to continue; if none is available, play continues. Ads require internet. The planned release is free with between-match ads, with no purchases or subscriptions.

Optional Android screen pinning can reduce accidental exits during same-screen play. System gestures and pinning vary by device; the game cannot guarantee that every phone blocks its menus.
