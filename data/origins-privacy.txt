Origins: Chain Reaction — Privacy policy
TripperDeeLabs · Updated November 20, 2026
Package: com.tripperdeelabs.shiftgrid
Contact: tripperdeelabs@gmail.com

Origins saves your current game, a previous-turn recovery copy, best score, settings and optional age group on your device. It does not require a game account or operate a gameplay server. Age selection uses a group, not a birth date; the application does not send this group to a studio server. Android backup may transfer eligible game data according to your device settings; purchase preferences are excluded from configured backup.

The release includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Players under 18 and players who decline to specify their age receive no ads; application code does not initialize the ad or consent SDK for them. Selecting an age group is not required to play. Adults receive ads only after a successful current privacy check. Applicable consent and opt-out choices are available under Settings > Purchases & privacy > Privacy choices. Failed privacy checks leave ads off. Non-personalized ad requests and a maximum G content rating are used; this does not mean no information is processed.

When ads are requested, Google may collect/share IP-derived approximate location, device/account identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google documents TLS transport. See https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure and https://policies.google.com/privacy . This app does not request GPS location, contacts, camera or microphone for puzzle play.

Google Play handles optional Remove Ads transactions and provides purchase/entitlement information to the app. We do not receive payment-card details. Purchase availability depends on Google Play. Under-18 and unknown-age players do not need this purchase. Restore is available through the same Play account. If you email support, your address and message are processed to respond; do not include children's personal information unnecessarily.

Local app data can be removed using Android app storage controls or uninstalling, which also removes local progress. Google account, ad and purchase information is managed through Google's controls and retention policies. Do not claim a studio deletion request automatically deletes Google's transaction records.
