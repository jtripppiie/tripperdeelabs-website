Bluetooth Drag Racing privacy policy

Updated November 9, 2026. This describes Bluetooth Drag Racing, package com.tripperdeelabs.bluetoothracing, by TripperDeeLabs.

Data stays on your device

The game stores your chosen driver name, local identifier, earned credits, owned cars, upgrades, paints, wheel finishes, tuning, settings, race totals, records and recorded ghost runs in app storage. There is no developer account, cloud-save service or developer analytics service. The app includes Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP privacy controls and Google Play Billing. Advertising and optional checkout are enabled in the release candidate, subject to age, privacy, Google Play availability and purchase-verification checks. The local identifier is used for game identity, not advertising.

Ghost recordings contain race timing, position and car state, not video, audio or location. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local game data. Android backup is disabled in the app manifest; device transfer behavior can vary.

Optional Bluetooth

The game requests Nearby devices access to connect to paired Android devices. Driver names, car builds, control inputs and race state are exchanged directly with your opponent over Bluetooth. They are not sent to a TripperDeeLabs server. Solo play does not require Bluetooth permission. The app does not request location, microphone, camera or contacts access.

Age groups and advertising

An optional neutral age-group choice is stored on the device; we do not request a birth date. Players under 18 and those who do not specify an age receive no ads. For adults, advertising requires a fresh Google UMP privacy check. Missing setup, errors or an ineligible age leave ads off. Release builds use production ad units only when the eligibility and privacy checks succeed. Development builds may use Google demonstration ads.

When enabled and permitted, only non-personalized, maximum-G-rated interstitials may appear after a completed race when leaving the results or starting another solo race. Connected Bluetooth rematches are not interrupted by advertising. Ads are never required to finish a race; offline or unavailable ads do not block continuing. Non-personalized advertising can still process IP addresses, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostics for delivery and fraud prevention. Advertising ID permission is removed. There is no mediation network in this candidate.

Use Settings → Privacy & purchases to change the age group, review applicable ad privacy choices, or read this policy. Under-18 and unknown-age players do not initialize the ad SDK or request ads. Applicable adult consent requirements and US-state privacy choices are handled through Google UMP. If the privacy check fails, ads remain off.

Optional purchases

Optional one-time products are a metallic paint pack (metallic_paint_pack), cosmetic gold wheel finish (track_wheel_pack) and the Apex GT car (apex_gt_car). Their US base price is $0.99 each; Google Play displays the applicable local price before purchase. Performance tire upgrades currently use earned credits only. Earned-credit alternatives remain available and do not charge money. Google Play handles payments; the developer does not receive card details. The app verifies signed purchase records, stores paid ownership separately from earned ownership, acknowledges completed purchases and supports restoration. Pending payments do not unlock items. Refund checks can remove paid ownership while preserving earned items.

Your choices and support

You can change your driver name and settings in the game and revoke Bluetooth permission in Android settings. Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com with privacy questions or support requests. We receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond; do not include passwords or payment details.

Website analytics are separate from the app and described in the website privacy policy.
