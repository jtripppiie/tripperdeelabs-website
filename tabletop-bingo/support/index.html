Tabletop Bingo support

Solo play

Choose Play Solo, then tap a called number to cross it off in green. Tap it again to undo. Repeat plays the last call again. Call Next Number draws the next ball once you have marked the current one. Auto turns the caller on or off.

Bluetooth games

The host chooses Host Game and keeps the game open. Guests choose Join Game and pick the host from the nearby list. The host approves each guest before cards are dealt. Up to five guests can join one host. If Bluetooth drops, tap Reconnect and ask the host to approve you again.

Saved games

Your solo card and called numbers are saved on your device, so you can resume after closing the app. Choose Keep Saved Game to continue, or Start New to begin a fresh card.

Ads, purchases and privacy

Open Settings for Remove Ads, Restore Purchases and privacy choices. Purchases require Google Play and the account used to buy them. Live purchase availability has not been verified for this development build.
