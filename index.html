Find a saved parking spot, explore local history, follow Alaska salmon counts, or keep selected photos in an encrypted vault. For downtime, explore arcade games; for your wrist, choose a watch face built around the information you want to see.

Phone apps

  • Parked

    Save your parking location from an Android home-screen widget. Parked uses your phone’s GPS to remember the spot without an internet connection.

    Open walking directions when you return, or share, replace or clear your saved pin. Directions open in your maps app and may require internet. GPS accuracy varies, especially inside garages.

    Explore Parked →

  • Local Lore

    Local Lore helps you explore the stories behind nearby places. Browse history, landmarks, nature notes and local legends on a map or in a list.

    Search another neighborhood before a trip and follow the linked sources to learn more. Internet access is needed to retrieve content and maps.

    Explore Local Lore →

  • Salmon Tracker

    Salmon Tracker displays public salmon passage counts for Kenai Peninsula rivers. Compare daily counts and season totals for the runs you choose to follow.

    An internet connection is needed for fresh counts and alerts. Previously downloaded reports remain cached when your connection drops. This is an unofficial client, and passage counts are not a catch forecast.

    Explore Salmon Tracker →

  • Private Eyes

    Private Eyes stores selected photos and files in an encrypted vault on your Android phone. Its camera can capture photos and short silent videos directly into the vault.

    The app works offline and has no internet permission. Imported originals remain outside the vault until you manage them separately. There is no recovery service or cloud backup, so a lost pattern or device can mean permanent loss.

    Explore Private Eyes →

  • Boss Button

    Boss Button places a movable button over your Android screen. Double-tap it to show a labeled privacy cover, then triple-tap the cover to return.

    Choose a built-in cover or an image on your device. It works offline and requires Display over other apps access. It provides a visual cover, not a device lock; your app stays open underneath.

    Explore Boss Button →

  • Mock Location

    Mock Location supplies test positions through Android’s Developer Options. Choose a point on a map, enter coordinates or recall a saved bookmark for authorized app testing.

    Use a fixed position or a movement preset. Map resources and address search require internet. Other apps can detect or reject mock locations; this utility does not bypass those checks.

    Explore Mock Location →

Phone games

  • RoboBall

    RoboBall is a basketball arcade game about timing your release and aiming at the hoop. Moving baskets, obstacles and changing gravity affect each shot.

    Practice your timing, earn stars and collect local trophies. Offline play keeps scores and progression on your device, with no account required.

    Explore RoboBall →

  • Moto Thrash

    Moto Thrash is a pixel-art motocross game with 24 tracks across four cups. Race computer opponents, balance your bike in the air and time your boost while watching engine heat.

    Build courses in six local save slots or attempt bus jumps in Stunt Show. Offline play is single-player, with progress and custom courses saved on your device.

    Explore Moto Thrash →

  • Captain Quack

    Captain Quack sends a duck through alien formations, asteroids and gravity wells. Use the touch joystick and fire button to steer through increasingly difficult runs.

    Pick up shields and weapon upgrades, then try to improve your local score. Offline play saves the top five scores and career ranks on your device.

    Explore Captain Quack →

    Canopy Quest is a retro jungle platformer with 90 connected rooms and 45 relics to find. Swing on vines, climb through passages and time your whip strikes.

    Plan your route with the field map and return to camps as you explore. Offline play stores progress locally; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local saves.

    Explore Canopy Quest →

Wear OS watch faces

  • Summit Line

    Summit Line places digital time over mountain scenery. Choose a landscape, accent color and optional step-goal ring, or use scenery that changes with fixed local-time periods.

    It displays data from your watch and compatible providers; it does not record hikes or provide GPS navigation. Wear OS 5 or newer is required. Personal-photo customization needs Wear OS 6 and a supported system flow.

    Explore Summit Line →

  • Launch Vector

    Launch Vector displays the next rocket launch countdown with time, weather and watch data. It includes launch status, an animated rocket and an ambient display.

    Install the Launch Vector Data Provider companion to refresh launch schedules. Schedule updates require internet. This is an independent design, not affiliated with or endorsed by SpaceX.

    Explore Launch Vector →

  • Kenai Fishing Report

    Kenai Fishing Report shows time alongside salmon counts, river temperature and tide information on a compatible Wear OS watch. Install the separate Kenai Report Data app on the same watch for these readings.

    Internet access is needed for updates. Requires Wear OS 5 or newer. The apps are independent of government agencies; reports can be delayed and do not determine whether fishing is permitted.

    Explore Kenai Fishing Report →

  • Amelia Navigator

    Amelia Navigator combines a navy aviation-style dial, brass details and digital time. A twin-engine aircraft approaches at the top of the hour; tap to replay the animation.

    Wear OS supplies available battery, steps, heart-rate and weather readings. Requires Wear OS 5 or newer. The compass-style ring is decorative, not a navigation instrument.

    Explore Amelia Navigator →

  • Dive Watch

    Dive Watch pairs an analog dial with luminous markers, a date window and a battery indicator inside the left marker. Choose Classic steel, Red night or Green lume.

    Customize the hands, dial finish and supported complication. Requires Wear OS 5 or newer. This is a decorative watch face: it does not add water resistance or provide dive-computer functions.

    Explore Dive Watch →

Wear OS companion apps

  • Kenai Report Data

    Kenai Report Data supplies public fishing-report data to compatible Wear OS complications. It is a separate watch app, not a watch face, and can work with Kenai Fishing Report or another compatible face.

    It fetches ADF&G counts, USGS readings and NOAA tide predictions over the internet and caches validated reports. Requires Wear OS 5 or newer. Check official rules and conditions; this independent app does not decide whether fishing is permitted.

    Explore Kenai Report Data →

About TripperDeeLabs

I’m the independent developer behind TripperDeeLabs. I make Android games, practical phone apps and Wear OS watch faces, with projects ranging from Alaska fish reports to retro arcade games.

Each app page explains what the app does, who it is for, and what stays on your device. Offline games save progress locally. Apps that use maps, public reports, or launch schedules need a connection for fresh information. Watch readings depend on the device and the providers you choose.

Read the app’s privacy policy for its specific permissions, storage, and external services. For questions, ideas, or support, email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com or visit the feedback page.
