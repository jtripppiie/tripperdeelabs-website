Stick Sprint — Offline stick-figure runner for Android

One stick figure. Three lanes. Plenty of questionable decisions. Chase your best score, collect coins and try to keep those knees moving in Stick Sprint, an offline arcade runner from TripperDeeLabs.

Find your rhythm

Tap the left or right side to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. Dodge obstacles and watch for trapdoors as the course narrows from three lanes to two or one. Park Circuit, Switchback Sprint and Trapdoor Trail offer 18 named sections across three repeating chapters, mixing slalom patterns, hurdle hops, duck gates and coin breaks. Runs end with a score summary and a comic quip. Coins and milestone rewards unlock runner styles.

Pick your atmosphere

Choose high-contrast monochrome, neon, moonlit, Blackout or the glowing Grid Run arena. Day turns to night, rain moves in, and monsoon sections bring rising water and slippery steering. Normal, Light Speed and Plaid Speed offer different paces.

Made for a quick run

Play in portrait or landscape on Android 13 or newer. The current development build works offline, with scores, coins, styles and settings saved on your device. It has no ads, account registration or in-app purchases.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download Stick Sprint now?

A public download is not available yet. Check this page for the Google Play release link when the game launches.

Does it need internet to play?

No. Gameplay is offline. Opening the website or sharing through another app uses that app and may require a connection.

Will my progress move to another phone?

There is no game account or cloud-sync service. Progress stays in local app storage; uninstalling or clearing storage can remove it.

How do I get help or send feedback?

Email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com with Stick Sprint in the subject. Include your device model, Android version and what happened. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.
