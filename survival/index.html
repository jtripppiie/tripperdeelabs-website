Survival

Your offline field guide.

Prepare for the outdoors with practical reference guides, supply checklists, downloadable regional maps and field tools.

Coming soon. Planned US$0.99 upfront, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Not yet released on Google Play.

Prepare before you leave reception

  • Read 102 offline chapters, search reference material and save favorites.
  • Plan your bug-out bag, food supplies, water and battery needs.
  • Choose and download regional OpenStreetMap-based maps over Wi-Fi, then view them offline.
  • Use a compass, available elevation estimates, saved waypoints and an on-screen breadcrumb trail.

Internet is needed for downloads, optional location lookups and external source links. Maps do not provide turn-by-turn routing or live conditions. Trail recording runs while Navigate is visible.

A guide, not a rescue service

Survival is informational reference material, not professional medical advice or a guarantee of safety. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

The included 1992 Army manual is historical and may contain outdated or dangerous advice. Plant photos cannot establish safe identification or edibility. Maps, GPS readings and facility records may be inaccurate or stale; a listed shelter may be closed. Use training, judgment and backup equipment. Contact local emergency services in an emergency.

Limited tablet testing has been completed. Independent expert safety review and broader field and Play-installed testing remain pending.
