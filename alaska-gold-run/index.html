Alaska Gold Run · Android

Submitted to Google Play · Changes in review

Offline Alaska runner game for Android

Alaska Gold Run is a three-lane Android runner set among snowy trails, railway tunnels, ice caves, and frozen lakes. Swipe to change lanes, jump over hazards, and slide under obstacles while collecting gold. It is for players who enjoy learning a route, reacting to changing terrain, and returning to beat their own score.

Submitted to Google Play

Alaska Gold Run has been submitted to Google Play. Its Play Console status is “Changes in review,” last updated October 24, 2026. Review is still pending, so there is no public download available here yet. This page will link to the public listing when it becomes available. The Android package is com.tripperdeelabs.alaskagoldrun.

Run, ski, and skate through Alaska-inspired scenery

The routes include a Whittier Tunnel-inspired railway, an ice cave, downhill skiing, and skating across frozen terrain. Outdoor night scenes feature an aurora. Watch for trains, wildlife crossing your path, and swooping eagles while choosing a clear lane. Spring Boots and timed double-score pickups add options during a run, and each environment gives you a different set of hazards to learn.

Earn rewards through play

Collect gold and work through missions to earn gear and outfit choices. Daily gifts and the Lucky Pan provide in-game rewards. The submitted release is designed as a pay-once offline game without advertising or in-app purchases. At base camp, adjust swipe sensitivity, graphics, sound, and vibration to make the controls and presentation comfortable on your phone.

Keep your progress on your device

Gameplay does not need an account or an internet connection after installation. Progress and settings stay locally on the device; the game has no analytics or online account service. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove your saved progress. The screenshots below are real desktop gameplay captures from release preparation, so the final Android display may differ.

Frequently asked questions

Is Alaska Gold Run available to download?

It has been submitted to Google Play and is marked “Changes in review,” last updated October 24, 2026. A public download link will be added when the listing is available.

Can I play without Wi-Fi?

Yes. The submitted release is designed for offline play after installation, with progress and settings stored on your device.

Are there ads or purchases for gold?

The submitted release has no ads or in-app purchases. Gold, outfits, and gear are earned through gameplay. The intended model is a one-time purchase.

Will an account restore my progress on another phone?

There is no game account or cloud-save service. Progress stays locally, and clearing storage or uninstalling can remove it.
