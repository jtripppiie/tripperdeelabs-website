Moments · Wear OS 6 or newer

In development · Public Play listing unavailable

Personal photo watch face for Wear OS

Moments is a photo watch face project for Wear OS 6, pairing a curved image panel with digital time and everyday data fields. It is intended for people who want a personal picture on their wrist without losing a readable time display. Public Google Play availability has not been confirmed, so the images here show a development build.

A picture beside the time

The design keeps the photo to one side of a large digital clock, with a centered weekday and date. Soft Colors, Silver, and Champagne themes change the information styling. The current build includes an optional minute ring, a battery percentage and fill, and four customizable complications. In always-on mode, a simpler layout leaves out the photo.

Use the watch platform to choose an image

Personal-photo selection is designed around the supported paired-phone and Wear OS system flow. That platform handles selection, cropping, transfer, and storage. Moments displays the selected image without sending it to TripperDeeLabs. Included sample images are illustrative, AI-generated artwork; the lake shown in these screenshots is a sample, not a real user’s personal photograph.

Understand the development status

The current project targets Wear OS 6 or newer. Emulator captures have been used to review its layout and editor options. Personal-photo transfer with a paired phone, persistence, and physical-watch provider behavior still need real-device validation. This preview is not a promise that every watch and phone pairing has been tested. No public price or download button is shown until a release listing is available.

Frequently asked questions

Can I install Moments from Google Play now?

The known public listing is currently unavailable. This page describes the project and its development screenshots, not an available purchase.

Does Moments upload my photos?

No. The face has no network permission or developer upload service. The watch platform and paired-phone software handle photo selection and transfer under their own privacy policies.

Can I choose the data fields?

The current build supports four customizable complications. Available readings and tap actions depend on the watch and providers; missing data can appear as dashes.

Is there a separate Moments phone app?

No separate TripperDeeLabs phone app is planned for this flow. Photo selection uses the supported watch manufacturer or Google companion software. Compatibility still needs validation before release.
