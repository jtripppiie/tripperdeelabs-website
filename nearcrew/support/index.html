A little help
getting connected.

Start with one host

Set your profile name and color. One Android phone chooses Create a room, then Bluetooth or Wi-Fi hotspot. Everyone stays nearby and uses the same connection type. Up to six participants can share the room, including the host. Keep the host powered on.

Join with the Android app

Choose Join someone nearby. For Bluetooth, enter the six-digit room PIN, find the host and follow Android pairing prompts. For Wi-Fi, use the host’s app-room QR invitation. Wait for the host to approve you. Do not share invitation codes publicly.

Join in a browser

The Wi-Fi host selects Help someone join → No app / iPhone. Scan the Wi-Fi QR first, then the Open chat QR. These are separate steps. Enter your name and wait for approval. Keep the webpage open. Automatic hotel-style captive-portal opening is not implemented. Browser chat is text-only and unencrypted; iPhone/Safari behavior still needs release qualification.

Delivery and reconnecting

Local only means nobody else received the message. Received by host does not mean every person received or read it. Check the connection indicator, move closer and use Reconnect or Retry. A restarted hotspot may require a fresh invitation. VPN or manufacturer battery settings can interfere with local networking. NearCrew does not relay across town or through other group members.

Photos, voice and meetings

Use the plus control for attachments. Voice notes are limited to 30 seconds; preview before sending. Tap your own confirmed text to edit it. The host can pin a meeting plan, which participants acknowledge; an edited plan needs a new acknowledgment. Browser guests cannot use native photos or voice features. Real multi-device photo, call and recording acceptance remains part of preview testing.

Locations are optional

Preview a one-time location before sending, or open Group locations and choose a timed sharing duration. Android location permission and notifications are needed for timed sharing. Confirm Start sharing separately. Stop from the app or notification. Maps displays a snapshot and may need downloaded maps or internet access. Positions can become stale; they do not prove someone is safe.

Read or delete saved chat

Saved conversation shows this phone’s bounded recent history. Delete saved messages and reconnection information while disconnected. Other participants keep their own copies. Starting or joining another room replaces the saved conversation. There is no chat export or unlimited archive.

Appearance and accessibility

Choose Light, Dark or Follow system in Appearance. Adjust chat text size or use Simple mode. Home icons return to the main screen; room controls explicitly distinguish navigating from leaving a connection.

Price and availability

Planned $0.99 US one-time paid download, with no ads or subscriptions. The app is not yet on Google Play. Child-audience controls and device qualification must be completed before public launch. This is not an emergency communication or remote child-monitoring service.

Contact
