Tower Breadcrumbs privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Developer: TripperDeeLabs. Package: com.tripperdeelabs.towerbreadcrumbs. This policy describes the current Android development build and its configured SDKs.

GPS and cellular observations

When you start recording, Tower Breadcrumbs stores your phone’s precise latitude and longitude, time, accuracy, reported cellular service, Android-validated cellular connectivity, available signal strength, carrier name and cell identifiers. These records remain in the app’s private local database. The app does not upload your trail to TripperDeeLabs or operate a cloud account or trail-sync service.

Navigation without recording uses a live GPS position to calculate a bearing and distance but does not add trail points. GPS runs only after you start it, with an ongoing Android notification, and stops when you choose Stop. The app does not start recording automatically after reboot.

Permissions

Precise location enables GPS breadcrumbs and permitted cellular readings. Phone-state access reads network information; the app does not read your call log, contacts or call audio or place calls. Notification permission supports the visible GPS status. Internet access is used by Google advertising, consent and purchase services. File import/export uses Android’s document picker so you choose the files.

Maps, cell files and exports

Imported offline maps and estimated cell-location CSV files are copied into app-private storage. Their original files remain where you stored them. Exported CSV files can contain sensitive location history, time, carrier and cellular identifiers. You choose the destination and whether to share them. Exports remain separate from the app and must be deleted separately.

Google advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Google’s SDKs may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), advertising/device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. SDK network data is encrypted in transit. The app does not intentionally supply its stored precise coordinates or cellular journal to the advertising SDK.

Regional consent and privacy choices depend on the app’s published consent configuration. Where required, advertising privacy choices can be reopened in Settings. Current preview builds use Google’s sample ad units; live ads and regional messages are not yet configured for release.

Purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time ad-removal purchase. The app receives purchase status, product identifiers and tokens to acknowledge purchases and restore access. It does not receive your payment-card details. Ad removal disables all banner and full-screen ads; it does not stop necessary Google Play purchase checks or erase information already processed by Google. Cached entitlement may persist until a later successful Play verification.

Retention and deletion

Recorded trails remain until you delete them in Logbook or remove app data. Imported map copies and cell estimates can be removed in Offline. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for this app. Uninstalling or clearing storage can remove local records permanently. TripperDeeLabs cannot recover trails kept only on your device. Original imports and exported files are outside this deletion. Google’s policies govern data held by its services.

Support and changes

If you contact tripperdeelabs@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use them to handle your request and retain them as needed for that purpose. Avoid sending sensitive trails unless necessary. Contact the same address about privacy or correspondence deletion. We will update this page when data practices change.
