Private Eyes: Photo Vault Lock · Android

Offline encrypted photo vault for Android

Private Eyes is an encrypted photo and file vault for Android. It stores selected files on your phone and includes a camera for photos and short silent videos captured directly into the vault. It is for people who want deliberate control over private copies without a developer account or an online storage service.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Import a copy, then manage the original

Choose files with Android’s file picker, review the import result, and open important copies to check them. Importing does not automatically hide or delete the source from Gallery, Downloads, or a cloud library. Optional original removal depends on the source provider and Android confirmation. Other copies can remain elsewhere, so review them separately instead of assuming one import protects every version.

Keep access and exports deliberate

AES-256-GCM encryption protects file contents, filenames, and stored thumbnails. The vault uses your pattern and device authentication, and locks when you leave the app or turn off the screen. Search and media filters help you browse stored items. Exports create readable copies outside the vault; choose a destination carefully and check what metadata remains. Direct private capture avoids making a Gallery original first.

Plan for local storage and recovery limits

Private Eyes has no internet permission, ads, analytics, account, or cloud service. Your vault is not uploaded to TripperDeeLabs, and the developer holds no recovery key. Losing your pattern, phone, or device keys can mean losing the contents permanently. Do not keep the only copy of irreplaceable files in the vault. Its protection does not promise forensic invisibility or independent security certification.

Frequently asked questions

What Android version is required?

Private Eyes requires Android 13 or newer and a device screen lock. Camera controls and media previews depend on your device and the file format.

Does importing remove a Gallery photo?

No. Importing creates a protected copy. Removal of the original is a separate, optional action, and other local or cloud copies may remain.

Can I recover my vault on another phone?

There is no portable encrypted backup, recovery service, or pattern reset. If the original phone still works, export and verify important files before changing devices.

Can I take videos inside the vault?

Yes. The built-in camera captures short silent videos directly into the vault. Videos are limited to two minutes and subject to storage and device limits.
