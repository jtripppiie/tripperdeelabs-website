Launch Vector Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Updated October 7, 2026

This policy covers the Launch Vector Wear OS watch face and Launch Vector Data Provider, developed by TripperDeeLabs.

Watch data

The face displays time, date, weather, battery, steps, and heart rate from Wear OS. Launch Vector does not send these values to developer-operated servers. Launch-site locations describe rockets, not your location; neither app requests device location access.

Launch-data service

The companion uses HTTPS to retrieve schedules from the TripperDeeLabs launch service. Automatic network requests occur every 15 minutes to six hours depending on launch proximity. You can also request a refresh. Requests do not include an account, advertising identifier, health readings, or device location. Our service and hosting provider may process IP addresses, request times, and user-agent metadata for delivery, security, reliability, and operational logging.

Local storage and deletion

The latest successful schedule and refresh times are cached on the watch. Clear the companion's app storage or uninstall it to remove this cache. Contact us about privacy questions or requests concerning server logs. Server-log retention depends on the hosting configuration; this policy does not promise immediate deletion of operational logs.

Advertising and sharing

The apps have no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or account system. TripperDeeLabs does not sell personal information. Hosting providers process request metadata to operate and protect the service. Wear OS providers and Google Play operate under their own privacy policies; Google handles purchases and downloads.

Children and changes

Launch Vector is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We update this policy when data practices change.

Contact

For privacy and support, email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. Launch Vector is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SpaceX.
