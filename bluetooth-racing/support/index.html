Bluetooth Drag Racing support

Your first run

Choose quarter mile or half mile on Home. Tap the mode selector to cycle Rookie, Street, Pro and Ghost. Hold Gas during the countdown to rev; the car stays staged until green. Keep Gas held to launch. Tap Shift as RPM enters the green shift band, or select Automatic in Settings. There is no brake pedal or false-start penalty.

Ghosts and records

Finish a computer race to save your first ghost for that car and distance. Ghost mode replays your fastest recorded run. A faster completed solo run replaces it. Quarter-mile and half-mile runs, car builds, weather and surface records are stored separately. The cars coast beyond the line before results; that animation does not change the measured finish time.

Garage and upgrades

Drag the garage car sideways to rotate it. Paint choices save immediately. Open Collection and Car Showroom for cars and cosmetic unlocks. Cars keep separate parts and final-drive settings. Performance Shop shows the six upgrade types. Dyno & Gearing compares simulated acceleration against RPM; its quarter-mile estimate is a game calculation, not real horsepower.

Bluetooth is in testing

Install the same build on both Android devices, enable Bluetooth and pair them in Android settings. Choose Host on one device and Join on the other. Allow Nearby devices access, choose the host and start when connected. Keep both games in the foreground. Physical two-device acceptance is still pending; report disconnections or synchronization problems with both device models and the app version.

Settings and troubleshooting

The gear opens settings; the Home icon returns. Choose shifting mode, speed units, control side, audio, frame rate and pedal target size. Solo gameplay works offline. Production ads and cash checkout are not enabled in this candidate. Settings → Privacy & purchases contains the age-group choice, ad privacy options, policy and purchase restoration. Under-18 and unspecified-age players receive no ads. Do not clear storage or uninstall if you want to keep your builds and records.

Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com with the app version, device model and steps to reproduce a problem.
