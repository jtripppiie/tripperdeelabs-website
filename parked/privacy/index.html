Parked · Privacy

Your parking pin stays yours.

Last updated August 9, 2026.

Information stored on your device

Parked: Find My Car stores one parking location locally on the user's device: latitude, longitude, save time, and Android's reported accuracy.

No developer collection

Parked does not create an account, display advertising, use analytics, transmit the saved location to TripperDeeLabs, or operate a backend. It does not request background-location access or internet access. TripperDeeLabs cannot retrieve the user's saved data.

Directions and sharing

When the user asks for directions or shares a location, Parked passes the destination to an installed map, browser, messaging, or other user-selected app governed by its own privacy policy.

Feedback diagnostics

Send Feedback prepares an editable report containing app and Android versions, device manufacturer and model, permission and location-service states, whether a pin exists, and installed widget count. It excludes coordinates and unique device identifiers and is never sent automatically.

Retention and deletion

The location remains until replaced, cleared, automatically reset after directions open, removed through Android app-storage controls, or deleted by uninstalling Parked. Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled.

Children

Parked is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children.

Contact

Questions can be sent through TripperDeeLabs support.
