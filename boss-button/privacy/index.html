Boss Button

Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

The short version: Boss Button has no advertising, analytics, account, tracking, or internet permission. Its settings and optional custom-image reference stay on your device.

Who publishes Boss Button

Boss Button is published by TripperDeeLabs. Privacy questions may be sent to feedback@tripperdeelabs.com.

Information handled by the app

Boss Button does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It stores local preferences needed for operation, including the selected cover, B-button appearance and positions, protected-dismissal choice, service state, and optional start-after-reboot choice.

The app does not inspect, record, transmit, or modify other apps. It does not capture the underlying screen, read browser history, enumerate installed apps, collect analytics, display advertising, or create accounts.

Custom images

A custom cover is selected through Android's document picker. Boss Button requests persistent read access only to the item you select and stores its Android document URI in private app preferences. It does not copy or upload the image.

Display over other apps and foreground service

Boss Button requests Display over other apps access solely to show the small draggable B. While enabled, it runs a user-visible foreground service with a persistent notification and Stop action. The overlay does not read or capture content beneath it. Start after reboot is off by default.

Biometric and device credentials

Optional protected dismissal uses Android's system authentication UI. Boss Button receives only success, failure, cancellation, or error status; it never receives biometric data, PINs, patterns, or passwords.

Retention and deletion

All app data remains on the device. Clear app storage or uninstall Boss Button to remove local preferences. TripperDeeLabs has no server copy to delete.

Children

Boss Button is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from children or any other user.

External services

The app contains no network functionality or third-party runtime SDKs. Visiting this site or emailing support occurs outside the app.

Changes

If data practices materially change, this policy will be updated before the corresponding release.
