Wonder Cascade Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Updated October 29, 2026

This policy covers Wonder Cascade (also titled Wonder Cascade: Match 3), including Android version 0.20.1 with Google AdMob, User Messaging Platform and Google Play Billing. The intended audience is ages 13 and older. The Android release uses real advertising units; development builds may use test ads. The game has no sign-in or game account.

Your puzzles and progress

The game stores your current puzzle, progress, scores, stars, badges, previously generated puzzle identifiers, tutorial progress, and sound and motion preferences on your device. These let you resume, retain achievements and avoid previously generated boards. You do not create a game account. TripperDeeLabs does not receive your saved puzzles or operate a cloud-save service. Puzzles work offline.

Browser feedback preview

The temporary browser preview at tripperdeelabs.com/wonder-cascade/play/ has no advertising SDK, purchases or analytics. Game files are cached in your browser, and progress and preferences are stored locally using browser storage. Clearing site data removes these saves; private browsing or blocked storage may prevent them from being retained. Browser progress is separate from Android progress.

Our website host receives ordinary connection information when delivering the preview. The Send feedback form keeps your text on the page until you choose to copy it or open an email draft. That draft includes your feedback, preview version, current level and screen, browser identification and display size. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send the email, the support-correspondence practices below apply.

Advertising and younger players

The Android integration uses Google AdMob to prepare occasional interstitial ads between completed levels. It does not display ads during puzzles, in tutorials, after losses or in Computer Run. Ads may be unavailable when offline or when privacy or purchase checks cannot complete.

We configure every player's ad requests with Google's teen age treatment, a G content rating and disabled ad personalization. Child-directed treatment is turned off. The app does not collect age or date of birth and has no age screen. Advertising-ID and Android advertising-services permissions are removed. We do not request contacts, camera, microphone or device-location permissions.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions, diagnostics and device or account-related identifiers to provide and measure ads and help prevent fraud. Removing advertising-ID permission does not prevent all network or device-data processing. Google's handling is subject to the age treatment and personalization settings applied by the app. We have not added a separate gameplay analytics or Firebase Analytics service.

See Google's privacy policy and its Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for information about Google's processing, security and retention.

Privacy choices

Wonder Cascade uses Google's User Messaging Platform to update advertising privacy requirements at launch and display a consent form when Google requires one. Ads remain disabled if the privacy update or form flow fails, requirements are unknown, or the SDK does not allow ad requests. When required, Settings → Privacy & about → Ad privacy opens Google's privacy-options form so you can review your choices. The app does not select consent choices for you. Teen treatment and non-personalized ad settings remain in effect.

Remove Ads purchases

Remove Ads is the only in-app purchase: a single, non-consumable Google Play purchase that removes all in-game ads while owned. The current localized price is shown before purchase. Checkout requires an active Play product and a successful purchase-ownership check. There are no subscriptions or purchases for boosters, moves, lives or currencies.

Google Play handles payment and any required account or payment authentication. TripperDeeLabs does not receive payment-card details. The app receives product, purchase-state, token and signed receipt information to verify and acknowledge the purchase and restore access. Signed purchase information is kept in the app's private local storage; we do not send it to a TripperDeeLabs server. Google handles billing records under its own policies.

Use Restore purchases with the same Google Play account on a supported installation. Verified ad-free access is retained offline; a successful Google Play query can update it if a purchase is no longer owned. Clearing local storage does not cancel a Google Play purchase. Restoring purchases does not restore puzzle progress.

Storage, security and deletion

On Android, saves, recovery copies, preferences and cached purchase receipts use the app's private storage. Android app backup is disabled. Google SDKs may also store privacy and service information locally. Local app data remains until it is removed through app or operating-system controls.

To delete local data, open Android Settings, select Wonder Cascade under Apps and clear storage, or uninstall the game. Menu names vary by device. This resets game progress; we cannot restore it from a server. Deleting the app does not delete records independently held by Google. Use Google's privacy and account controls or contact Google concerning those records. Google's SDK disclosures describe encryption in transit; no storage or transmission method is guaranteed risk-free.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that correspondence to respond, investigate issues and follow up. Your email provider and ours process correspondence for delivery and storage. The game does not automatically attach your saved puzzle, purchase receipt or device files.

We retain support correspondence as needed to address your request, related follow-up or applicable obligations. You or a parent or guardian can request deletion of correspondence by emailing feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support. Delete local progress using device controls; Google handles requests about information it holds.

Website and external services

Opening this policy, an email app or another external service is separate from playing a puzzle. Our website host receives connection information when serving this page; see our website privacy policy. External services have their own privacy practices.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, support or requests concerning support correspondence, contact TripperDeeLabs at feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. Changes will be posted here with an updated date and applicable app version.
