Website hosting

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Requests necessarily reach the hosting provider with connection information such as your IP address. GitHub states that Pages visitor IP addresses are logged for security, even without a GitHub login. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your optional analytics choice

Google Analytics and the homepage visitor counter stay off until you select Accept analytics. Declining does not prevent reading the site or contacting us. A preference named tdl-cookie-consent-v2 is stored in your browser's local storage so we can remember your choice. If storage is unavailable, the choice applies to the current page only. JavaScript-disabled browsing does not load these optional services.

Use Cookie settings to change your choice. Declining after accepting disables further Google Analytics measurement on the current page, cancels a pending counter request where possible, removes accessible Google Analytics cookies, and reloads the page when the preference can be saved. Previously transmitted requests cannot be recalled. Other open pages using these controls respond to a saved change too.

Google Analytics

If you accept, we load Google's analytics script to understand website usage. It can process page visits, browser/device information, referral information and analytics identifiers in cookies. We configure Google signals and advertising personalization signals off. Google receives the network requests; see Google's privacy policy. This is website measurement, not analytics inside Private Eyes.

Homepage visitor counter

If you accept and visit the homepage, your browser requests an increment from api.counterapi.dev for the shared site counter. The request includes the counter address and a cache-busting timestamp. We omit browser credentials and the referrer header. As with any network request, the provider receives connection information such as your IP address and browser headers. The displayed number counts consenting visits, not unique people.

Contact and feedback

Contact links open your email app. The feedback page can prepare the product name and browser user-agent string, which may contain browser and operating-system details. Review or delete these before sending. Messages go to feedback@tripperdeelabs.com through your email provider and ours, and are used to respond to your request. Do not send private app files, vault patterns or phone credentials.

Retention and choices

The local consent preference remains until you change it or clear browser storage. Hosting and analytics providers handle their records under their own policies and service settings; this page does not promise a fixed deletion period for their records. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable obligations. Changing consent stops future optional measurement; it does not automatically delete earlier provider records.

Questions and updates

TripperDeeLabs publishes this website. Email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com with privacy questions. We will update this page when the site's data handling changes.
