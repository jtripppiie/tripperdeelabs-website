Salmon Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

The short version: Salmon Tracker requires no account and contains no advertising or behavioral analytics. TripperDeeLabs does not receive, sell, or share your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Followed projects, notification preferences, synchronization status, and downloaded fish counts are stored locally on your device. Android backup is disabled for Salmon Tracker. You can clear this information through Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Official fish-count requests

Salmon Tracker connects directly to the Alaska Department of Fish and Game Fish Counts service to retrieve public information. As with any web request, that government-operated service may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and user agent under its own practices.

Salmon Tracker does not send advertising identifiers, analytics events, precise location, contacts, photos, or user-entered content.

Notifications and background work

Notifications are generated locally from saved count updates. You may disable notifications or automatic background synchronization from the app's Settings screen. Notification permission is optional.

Government affiliation

Salmon Tracker is an independent, unofficial app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the State of Alaska or the Alaska Department of Fish and Game. Source counts may be preliminary and remain subject to revision.

Optional feedback diagnostics

When you choose Send feedback, Salmon Tracker prepares an editable email containing the app and Android versions, device manufacturer and model, and notification and background-synchronization settings. It excludes downloaded fish counts, followed projects, synchronization timestamps, and unique device identifiers. Nothing is sent automatically.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact TripperDeeLabs or open an issue on the Salmon Tracker support page.

Changes to this policy

If Salmon Tracker's data practices materially change, this page and its effective date will be updated before the corresponding app release.
