Wonder Cascade Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Effective October 25, 2026

This policy covers the offline Android version 0.10.1 of Wonder Cascade, also titled Wonder Cascade: Match 3. TripperDeeLabs develops the game and is the contact for questions about this policy.

Information used by the game

Wonder Cascade stores your current puzzle, progress, scores, stars, earned badges, previously generated puzzle identifiers, tutorial progress, and sound and motion preferences locally on your device. This information lets you resume a puzzle, retain your achievements, avoid previously generated boards, and keep your chosen settings.

This version works offline. It does not require an account, transmit gameplay data to TripperDeeLabs or other parties, or include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchase services. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or advertising ID.

Storage, security, and deletion

On Android, the game stores its saves and recovery copies in the app's private storage. It does not operate a cloud-save service or keep server copies of your progress. Android app backup is disabled in this version.

Saved progress and preferences remain on your device until the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. To delete them, open Android Settings, select Wonder Cascade under Apps, and clear its storage, or uninstall the game. Menu names vary by device. Deleting this data resets your progress; TripperDeeLabs cannot restore it from a server.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to provide. We use that correspondence to answer your question, investigate a reported issue, and handle related follow-up. Your email provider and ours process the correspondence as part of delivering and storing email. The game does not automatically attach your saved puzzle or device files.

We retain support correspondence as needed to address your request and related follow-up or meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion by emailing feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. This concerns correspondence you have sent us; locally stored game progress can be deleted using your device's controls.

Advertising and future versions

Version 0.10.1 does not include Google AdMob or send advertising requests. Advertising is planned for a future version. Before enabling it, we will update this policy to describe the actual advertising services, information they process, purposes, sharing, and available privacy choices. This offline-version policy does not describe an ad-supported release.

Website and external services

Visiting this website is separate from playing the offline game. Our hosting provider receives connection information when it serves this page; the website privacy policy explains website data handling. Opening an email app or another external service subjects that activity to the service's own privacy practices.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, support, or requests concerning support correspondence, contact TripperDeeLabs at feedback@tripperdeelabs.com.

Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date and applicable app version. Any disclosures or consent controls needed for new data practices will accompany the relevant app update.
