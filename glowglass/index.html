Glowglass:
Light Puzzles

Turn. Connect.
Light it up.

Turn a mirror. Move a piece of glass. Watch cyan, gold and violet find their way home. Sixty optical puzzles invite you to slow down, look closer, and think a few turns ahead.

Explore the puzzles How to play

In development. Planned as a $0.99 one-time purchase, with no ads or in-app purchases. Not yet available on Google Play; final regional pricing may differ.

Simple to touch. Satisfying to untangle.

Let the light do the talking.

Tap glass to rotate it. Drag a dotted mirror into an empty socket. The beam changes immediately, so every experiment gives you something to think about. Your goal is to illuminate every receiver with its labeled color at the same time.

There is no timer, energy meter or penalty for trying again. Undo takes you back one move; free, two-stage hints point toward a solution without making the move for you.

Six chapters. Sixty different connections.

First light

Learn by touching

Meet mirrors, splitters, color filters and three-way prisms in ten introductory puzzles.

Reflections

Look further ahead

Follow longer paths and discover how a small turn changes the whole route.

Echoes

Think in two directions

A splitter carries light straight ahead and around a corner. Both paths matter.

Spectrum

Give each color a home

Route cyan, gold and violet through prisms, mirrors and filters.

Moving light

Change your perspective

Move glass between sockets, then choose the angle that completes the connection.

Resonance

Bring it all together

Linked turns and movable mirrors combine into the most involved puzzles.

Your pace. Your space.

All chapters are open to explore. The game remembers unfinished boards and your personal best move counts on this device. Return for a short break or work through a whole chapter.

Color labels accompany the light, sound is optional, and animated light can be switched off. The game works offline without an account, advertising, analytics or a gameplay server.

Progress stays local: clearing app storage or uninstalling removes it. There is no cloud restore.
