A SMALL TASK SHOULD STAY SIMPLE

Tap. Speak. Check the details.

  1. Open Just Remind Me or tap its home-screen widget.
  2. Speak a supported command with a clear date and time.
  3. Review the details and confirm. For alarms and calendar events, finish in the app that opens.

“Remind me to call Steve tomorrow at 9 AM.”

Or try “Remind me in 30 minutes to check the oven,” “Wake me up tomorrow at 7 AM,” or “Put a dentist appointment on my calendar next Tuesday at 10 AM.”

Useful, without another subscription.

The planned release is a one-time $0.99 US purchase, with no advertising or paid AI service. Final local prices and availability will appear on Google Play at launch.

Built around Android, with clear limits.

Speech uses your installed Android recognition service. On-device recognition is preferred when available; language downloads or an internet connection may be needed. Your recognition provider may process audio under its own privacy policy.

Reminder delivery depends on notification permission, alarm access and Android device settings. Countdown timers and conversational follow-up questions are still in development and are not included in the current preview. Physical-device reliability testing is ongoing.
