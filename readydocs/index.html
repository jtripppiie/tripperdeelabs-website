ReadyDocs

Hand over the document. Keep your phone locked.

Show a selected PDF or image to someone on your phone without opening your other apps. ReadyDocs displays your selected files in a read-only viewer over Android’s secure lock screen.

In development · Android 11 and newer

How to use ReadyDocs Privacy policy

Choose what to show

Save up to three documents in your encrypted vault. ReadyDocs can guide you through photographing both sides of a driver’s license, a health insurance card, or a car insurance card. You can also import a PDF or image. Choose the files for this handoff and let ReadyDocs lock the phone after one-time setup, or lock it yourself. Wake the phone without unlocking. The recipient can navigate the selected PDFs and images. Exiting the viewer returns them to Android’s lock screen.

Set up a separate emergency card

Add the name, emergency contact or medical notes you choose to make visible on the lock screen. On phones that support lock-screen widgets, the optional ReadyDocs Emergency widget opens the card directly. There is also a Quick Settings tile for phones that allow access to tiles while locked.

Keep your files on your device

ReadyDocs uses Android Keystore and works offline. It has no account, ads or cloud sync. Your original files stay with the provider you selected.
