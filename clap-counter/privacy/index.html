Clap Counter Privacy Policy

Updated November 21, 2026 Publisher: TripperDeeLabs | Contact: tripperdeelabs@gmail.com

Microphone

Clap Counter requests microphone permission only for sound counting or calibration. It processes short audio samples in memory to estimate sound intensity and detect events. The application does not write raw audio recordings to storage or transmit microphone samples. Manual counting works without microphone permission. Listening stops at the 15-second round deadline, or when paused, stopped, reset, interrupted or moved to the background. Android microphone permissions can be revoked in system settings.

Local data

The app stores session counts, durations, timestamps, names, mode and sensitivity, plus settings and ad/purchase state on the device. The app does not ask for or retain an age group; obsolete local age preferences are removed at launch. All ages get the same child-directed advertising settings. Cloud backup and device-transfer backup are excluded by the app's backup rules. Saved sessions can be renamed or deleted, and all history can be cleared after confirmation. Android's normal app storage controls can remove app data. A local preference records dismissal of the optional welcome message so it is not shown again on later launches.

One-time welcome message

On the first launch after installation or this update, Clap Counter can show a dismissible, one-time welcome message inviting you to explore our other apps, leave a Google Play review or send feedback. These actions are optional and require the grown-up check. The prompt does not automatically open a website, submit a review, send an email or transmit information. If you choose an external destination, its own privacy practices apply.

Sharing and support

After a grown-up check, sharing opens Android's Sharesheet with a text summary or app link. The user chooses a destination; that destination has its own privacy practices. Avoid putting personal information into session names. Support opens an email draft to tripperdeelabs@gmail.com; nothing is sent automatically. Information voluntarily sent in support email is used to respond.

Advertising and consent

The release uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) interstitials after eligible saved sessions or confirmed resets, with a shared cooldown. Timer expiry alone does not show an ad. Requests use child-directed treatment and a maximum general-audience content rating for every user. Advertising-ID and Android AdServices identifier/attribution/topics permissions are removed. These restrictions do not mean Google receives no data: the SDK may process IP address/approximate location, app interactions, diagnostic information and other data described in Google's SDK disclosure. The Google UMP integration is tagged under age of consent. Privacy choices appear when required by UMP.

Purchases

The one-time Remove Ads product is processed by Google Play. TripperDeeLabs does not receive payment-card details. The app receives purchase state and a signed purchase record, verifies the local signature/product/package, acknowledges confirmed purchases and queries Play to restore or reconcile ownership. A cached local entitlement can persist while offline; refunds/revocations are reconciled when a successful Play query reports current ownership. Purchase and restore actions require a grown-up check. The release contains the app-specific public verification key; purchase availability and localized price depend on Google Play.

Children

The app is intended for all ages, including children. It does not require an account, precise location, contact list, birthday or raw-audio upload. A local grown-up challenge helps prevent accidental external actions; it is not identity verification or legal parental consent. Parents can contact the support address about privacy questions.
