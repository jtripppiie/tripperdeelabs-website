Tracker: Nearby Signals — Privacy Policy

About this policy

Tracker: Nearby Signals is an Android app by TripperDeeLabs for observing nearby radio broadcasts and reviewing encounters. This policy explains the app’s local data, advertising, purchases and optional sharing. Last updated November 5, 2026.

Information kept on your device

Tracker stores observed Bluetooth identifiers, broadcast names and classifications, signal strength, encounter times, and trusted-device choices locally. It also displays Wi-Fi, classic Bluetooth and cellular information made available by Android. Broadcast identifiers may change and do not establish a person’s identity or prove tracking.

Location and permissions

Location permission supports radio discovery on Android. If you enable Save my phone location, recent phone coordinates and reported accuracy may be saved with future Bluetooth encounters. This setting is optional and off by default. Coordinates describe the observing phone, not the broadcasting device. Tracker does not request background-location permission. Nearby-device permissions support Bluetooth and Wi-Fi discovery. Notification permission supports optional monitoring and encounter alerts.

Advertising and consent

Tracker uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location; app and advertising interactions; diagnostic information; and device or account identifiers, including advertising and app-set identifiers where available. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. Tracker does not intentionally send its radio journal or saved precise phone coordinates to the advertising SDK. Advertising requires an internet connection. Where applicable, Google’s User Messaging Platform provides consent choices; when required, advertising privacy choices can be reopened in Settings. Availability depends on region and the configured consent messages.

Purchases

Google Play handles the optional one-time remove-ads purchase where available. Tracker receives purchase status, product identifiers and purchase tokens to acknowledge purchases and restore access. Tracker does not receive your payment-card details. Ad-free access stops further banner and full-screen ad requests; it does not delete information previously processed by Google. Cached purchase access may remain until the next successful Google Play verification.

Exports and maps

CSV and JSON exports can contain radio identifiers, names, timestamps, signal readings and optional saved phone coordinates. You choose where exported files are stored and whether to share them. Opening a saved observation in a map app passes its phone coordinates to the selected map service, whose privacy policy applies. Exported files are separate from the app’s history and must be deleted separately.

Monitoring and storage

Optional Bluetooth monitoring runs with a visible Android notification and can be stopped in Settings. Android may interrupt or delay scans. TripperDeeLabs does not operate a cloud journal or receive the app’s radio observations or saved phone coordinates. No app account is required. Android backup is disabled for Tracker.

Retention and deletion

You can delete saved encounter history and trusted-device records in Settings. Local records remain until deleted through the app, applicable retention settings, Android app-storage controls, or uninstalling. Google’s retention and deletion practices apply to information processed by its services. Contact us about privacy questions; TripperDeeLabs cannot retrieve a journal that remains solely on your device.

Age audience

Tracker is intended for teens and adults ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Advertising and consent configuration must account for applicable age and regional requirements.

Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change and will update the date on this page. For privacy questions, contact feedback@tripperdeelabs.com.
