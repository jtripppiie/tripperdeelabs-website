Cosmic Adventures

Coming soon to Google Play · Currently in testing

Take flight

Dodge asteroids, battle vertical alien formations, and escape gravity wells in an endless arcade adventure. Each run grows more challenging, with a Void Dreadnought boss every fifth level.

Collect, survive, and beat your best

Pick up shields, rapid fire, wide shots, and extra lives. Clear entire alien formations for bonus points and build your local top-five scoreboard and career rank.

Designed with a touch joystick, a dedicated fire button, adjustable music and sound settings, and pause controls. The game pauses when you leave the app and waits for you to resume.

Play offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, and no account required in the game. Scores and settings stay on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress.
