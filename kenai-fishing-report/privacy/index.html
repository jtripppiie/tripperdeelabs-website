Kenai Fishing Report Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Tripper Dee Labs provides Kenai Fishing Report (com.tripperdeelabs.kenaifishingreport) and Kenai Report Data (com.tripperdeelabs.kenaifishingreport.data) for Wear OS. This policy covers both apps. They are independent and do not represent or have endorsement from ADF&G, USGS, NOAA, the State of Alaska, or any government entity.

The watch face

The face uses Watch Face Format to display time, date, battery, moon phase, optional native steps and heart rate, and selected complication values on your watch. It has no executable application code, network client, analytics or advertising SDK, account system, location tracker or workout recorder. It does not transmit these readings to Tripper Dee Labs. Available health data and permissions are controlled by Wear OS and your device or selected provider. It does not initiate heart-rate measurements and is not a medical device.

Public fishing data and network requests

The separate data app requests fixed public reports directly from ADF&G, USGS and NOAA over HTTPS. Requests contain source/station identifiers and requested dates, not your GPS location, health readings, name, email, contacts, advertising identifier, or an app account. We do not operate a server receiving these requests and do not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

As with ordinary internet requests, receiving agencies can see connection metadata such as your IP address, request time, requested URL and an app User-Agent. Their websites describe logging and use of technical information; information inferred from an IP address may include a broad geographic area. We do not control agency logs or their retention. This means the data app is not an entirely offline app and we do not claim that no information ever leaves the device.

Review the ADF&G privacy policy, USGS privacy policy, and NOAA privacy policy.

Storage and your choices

Validated public reports, their fetch times, and update/failure status are cached in the data app's private storage on your watch. Clear its app storage or uninstall it to remove the cache. The apps disable Android backup and device transfer of their private data. You can remove or change complications, choose the native fitness row, manage platform/provider permissions, or uninstall the apps. No app account is created, so there is no account-deletion workflow. Removing a local cache does not delete government-service network logs.

External services

Official source links may open a browser when available. Source sites, Google Play, Wear OS, your watch manufacturer and other chosen complication providers operate under their own policies. Google Play handles watch-face purchases; these apps do not receive your payment-card information. Source websites are not embedded in an app-owned tracking web view.

Support

If you email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve the request. Please do not include sensitive health data or unnecessary personal information. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when practices change and will revise its effective date. For privacy questions, contact feedback@tripperdeelabs.com.
