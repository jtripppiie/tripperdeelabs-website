Take private photos without adding them to Gallery

Use the Private Eyes camera to capture directly into an offline encrypted vault and understand its limits.

Reviewed October 26, 2026 · TripperDeeLabs

The Private Eyes camera captures directly into the vault instead of publishing a new photo to Gallery. This avoids the extra step of importing a camera-roll image and deciding whether to delete its original.

Capture directly into the vault

  1. Unlock Private Eyes using the device verification and vault pattern.
  2. Open Camera and grant camera access if Android asks.
  3. Frame the shot. Use supported zoom and tap-to-focus controls as needed.
  4. Take the photo, then review it inside the vault.
  5. Lock the vault when you finish.

New photos and videos captured by the private camera have identifying metadata removed before encryption. Imported files follow a different rule: their original bytes are preserved, including metadata they already contain.

Photos and short silent video

The current camera supports photos and short silent videos. Video recording is limited to two minutes and 120 MiB, and available memory can reduce the limit or prevent recording. Camera modes and quality choices depend on the device. This is not an unlimited video recorder.

Sharing creates a separate copy

Exporting a file produces an unencrypted copy outside the vault. For supported JPEG photos, the cleaned export option removes embedded metadata and re-encodes the image; the stored original remains unchanged. Check the exported result before sending it.

Metadata removal cannot hide information visible in the picture itself. Faces, addresses on paper, reflections and recognizable surroundings can still disclose information.

Before you rely on the camera

Try a harmless capture on your phone and confirm the result opens. Keep sufficient free space. Private Eyes has no cloud backup or recovery service, so direct capture makes a preservation plan especially important for irreplaceable photos.
