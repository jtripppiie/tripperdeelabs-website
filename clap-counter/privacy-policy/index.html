Clap Counter Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · TripperDeeLabs

This policy describes the planned privacy practices of Clap Counter, an Android app for counting claps and short sound events. The app is under development; details will be updated to match the released version. TripperDeeLabs does not require an account to use the app.

Microphone access and audio processing

Clap Counter requests microphone permission when you choose sound detection. The intended implementation analyzes audio on your device to count sound events. Raw microphone audio is not intended to be recorded to files, uploaded to TripperDeeLabs, or sent to advertisers. Microphone capture stops when detection stops. Manual counting does not require microphone permission. You can revoke microphone access in Android Settings.

Counting sessions and settings

Counts, session names, dates, durations, detection settings, and preferences are intended to be stored locally on your device. The app does not require cloud synchronization or an account. You can delete saved sessions in the app. Device backups may preserve app data depending on the final Android backup configuration and your device settings.

Advertising

The free version is planned to use Google AdMob for occasional full-screen interstitial advertisements between completed sessions, not while actively listening. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. These data practices are governed by Google's policies and the applicable consent choices. Removing ads disables in-app ad display but does not erase information previously processed by Google.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures. Where required, the app will use Google's consent tools to present advertising privacy choices.

One-time Remove Ads purchase

An optional, non-consumable Remove Ads purchase is planned through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment information; TripperDeeLabs does not receive your payment card details. The app may process purchase status, product identifiers and transaction tokens to verify and restore ad-free access. Google retains its own billing records under its policies.

Sharing and reviews

The optional Share App button opens Android's sharing interface, allowing you to choose whether and where to share a link. The optional Rate App feature opens Google Play or uses Google's official in-app review flow. Google processes review activity under its own privacy practices.

Security and retention

The app is designed to keep sound analysis and counting history on-device. Locally saved sessions remain until you delete them or remove app data, subject to device backup behavior. Third-party advertising and billing providers retain data according to their policies. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children's privacy

Clap Counter is not specifically directed to children under 13. The app is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information directly. Advertising configuration and any age-related requirements will be reviewed before release.

Your choices

You may deny or revoke microphone permission, use manual counting, delete session history, manage available advertising consent choices, purchase the ad-free version, or uninstall the app. Advertising and purchase records held by Google are subject to Google's controls and policies.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy as app features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when revisions are published. This policy must be reviewed against the final release before the app is submitted to Google Play.
