Parked · Support

Let’s get you back to the car.

Quick answers for saving a pin, finding the car, and keeping the widget working.

The widget does not appear

Long-press an empty area of the Android home screen, choose Widgets, find Parked: Find My Car, and add the 2×1 widget. Launcher menus vary by phone.

Park Here cannot get a location

Confirm that Android Location Services are enabled and that Parked has approximate or precise foreground-location permission. GPS accuracy is usually better outdoors than inside a parking structure.

Find My Car reset the widget

That is expected. Once walking directions open successfully, Parked clears the old location and returns the widget to Park Here.

I need to replace or clear a pin

Tap App on the widget. The companion screen provides explicit controls for replacing, sharing, or clearing the saved location.

Send useful feedback

Open Parked and tap Send Feedback. The app prepares a diagnostic report without coordinates or unique device identifiers. You can review and edit it before choosing a sharing app.
