Untangle Trails

Find a clearer path, one peg at a time.

Move numbered pegs across an ivory puzzle board until every cord is clear. Brass trim, ocean-teal details and a nautical backdrop give this quiet logic game a physical feel.

In development for Android. Not yet available on Google Play.

Untangle your way through 100 puzzles

Work through ten campaign chapters, earn up to three stars and revisit puzzles to improve your move count. Any valid arrangement with zero crossings wins; there is no single required final layout. Fixed gold pegs add another planning challenge.

Play at your pace

Start untimed, or enable the optional timer. Free Play offers Easy, Standard and Expert scrambles separately from campaign progress. Use hints, undo and restart when you need another approach.

Numbered pegs, optional cord patterns, board guides, vibration and reduced-motion settings help you make the board your own. Puzzle gameplay works offline and progress stays on your device, without a player account.

Ads and purchases

The Android build includes interstitial ads between rounds, scheduled after every two completed non-tutorial puzzles when an ad is available. Ads do not interrupt a move. An optional permanent Remove Ads purchase is planned through Google Play; checkout and regional prices will be available with the store release.
