Survival support

Read offline

Open Library to browse guides or use Home search. Sources & Safety in More contains the persistent safety notice, source links, software licenses and offline privacy policy.

Download a map

While online, open Navigate → Offline regions. Choose a region, check its size and coverage, then confirm Download. Wi-Fi only is selected by default. Once saved, choose Open offline map. Update and Delete manage that device’s map copy without deleting waypoints.

Keep enough free space for a replacement download and the existing map. If a provider is unavailable, retry later; already-saved maps remain accessible. Download and check your maps before leaving reception.

Location, compass and elevation

Allow location only when you want location features. Indoor reception and device sensors affect accuracy. Elevation can be unavailable or inaccurate; the app labels the reference and available accuracy. Straight-line bearings do not identify a safe walking route. Breadcrumb recording stops when Navigate is not visible.

Privacy and deletion

Online nearby-place refresh asks before sending coordinates to providers. Manual region selection does not need GPS. Delete downloaded regions, waypoints and trails using the app’s controls. Cancel active downloads before clearing app storage. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local app records; there is no remote app account.

Price and availability

Planned US$0.99 upfront, for adults 18+, with no ads or in-app purchases. Survival has not been released on Google Play. Purchase and refund support will use Google Play once released.

Get help or report a correction

Email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com with the app version, Android version and a non-sensitive description. Do not send precise locations or personal medical details. This inbox is not an emergency service.
