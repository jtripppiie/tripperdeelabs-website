Two states. Two taps.

A parking reminder without the clutter.

PARK

Tap Park Here

Save the phone's current foreground location when you leave the car.

RETURN

Tap Find My Car

Open walking directions to the saved pin, then reset the widget for next time.

HOME

Stay on the home screen

The widget handles the everyday flow without dropping you into a complicated map interface.

CONTROL

Open the full app

A separate App button provides sharing, replacement, and manual clearing controls.

PRIVATE

Local-only storage

No account, ads, analytics, backend, or background-location permission.

FLEXIBLE

Works with your maps app

Directions open in Google Maps when available, with a browser-compatible fallback.

Privacy by design

Your parking pin stays on your phone.

Parked stores one latitude, longitude, save time, and accuracy value in private app storage. The developer cannot retrieve it. Coordinates leave Parked only when you explicitly open directions or share the pin with another app.

Location accuracy varies by phone, buildings, satellite visibility, and parking structures. Parked is a convenient parking aid, not an emergency or personal-safety tool.
