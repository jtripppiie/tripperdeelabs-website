RoboBall support

Something missed, moved, disappeared, or did not fit your screen? Send us the details and we will investigate.

Contact

Email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. RoboBall has no account system, so include the level name, device model, Android version, and what happened.

Send a support report

Before reporting a gameplay problem

  • Rotate the device to landscape.
  • Use the left-side slider to aim and the right-side Shoot button to release the ball.
  • Try Restart Level from Pause if a moving element appears out of position.
  • Check Settings for sound, haptics, motion, contrast, HUD size, and aim-guide controls.

Progress and local data

Player profiles, progress, settings, and high scores are stored only on the device. They cannot be recovered from TripperDeeLabs because they are never uploaded. Reset Progress and Reset Scores are available in Settings.

Privacy requests

RoboBall does not hold player data on a server. Local data can be removed through the in-game reset controls, Android's Clear storage control, or by uninstalling the game. For questions about an email sent to TripperDeeLabs, contact us from the same email account.
