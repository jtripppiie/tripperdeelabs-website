Alaska Gold Run Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Updated October 23, 2026

Alaska Gold Run is an offline game by TripperDeeLabs. This release has no accounts, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, chat, or online leaderboards. The game does not collect or send personal data to us or third parties.

Scores, earned gold, outfits, missions, tutorial progress and settings are stored only in the app's local storage. They are not uploaded or backed up by the game. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes this progress. A device reset may also erase it.

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone. Optional vibration is controlled in Settings. No personal information is requested from children.

If you voluntarily email support, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information. Google Play separately handles its own store and device services under Google's privacy policy.

Support: feedback@tripperdeelabs.com Developer: TripperDeeLabs

This notice describes the offline release only. It will be updated before any online service, advertising or in-app purchases are added.

This policy describes the game. Website visits are covered by the website privacy policy. This page does not load analytics scripts.
