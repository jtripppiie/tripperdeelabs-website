JUST REMIND ME · PRIVACY

Your reminders.
Your choices.

Updated November 5, 2026. Publisher: TripperDeeLabs. This policy describes the current development app, package com.tripperdeelabs.justremindme.

Speech and microphone access

Microphone permission is used when you start voice input. The app prefers Android's on-device speech recognizer when available; otherwise it uses the installed system recognizer. The recognition provider may send audio to its servers or require downloaded language support. Its own privacy policy and settings apply. Just Remind Me does not operate a speech server or call a paid AI API, and its own code does not save raw audio recordings.

Recognized text is used in memory to interpret your request and display confirmation. Confirmed reminder titles and scheduling information are saved locally. Denying microphone access prevents voice input. You can revoke access in Android Settings.

Reminder data and backup

The app stores reminder titles, due times, recurrence settings and timezone information in its private Android storage. Delivered one-time entries are removed; recurring entries advance to their next occurrence. There is currently no in-app list or individual delete control. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes local app data and can prevent scheduled reminders from firing.

Android backup is enabled in this build. Depending on your device and account settings, Android may back up or transfer app data through the system backup provider. This is separate from a TripperDeeLabs cloud-sync service; the app has no such service. Manage backups in Android and account settings.

Clock and Calendar

When you confirm those actions, the app passes the requested title and time to an installed Clock or Calendar app. That app handles saving, alerts and any account synchronization under its own settings and privacy policy. Just Remind Me does not request calendar database read/write permission and cannot verify whether you saved the external action.

Permissions and background work

Notifications show reminder alerts. Exact-alarm access allows more precise scheduling; without it Android may delay delivery. The boot-completed permission supports restoring future reminders after a restart. Alarm permission allows opening the Clock app's alarm flow. The app declares no foreground service, location, contacts, advertising-ID or internet permission. System recognition apps can independently use the network.

No ads or app analytics

The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, subscription or in-app purchase integration. The intended paid download is processed by Google Play. TripperDeeLabs does not receive your payment-card details through this app. No app account is required.

Support and this website

If you email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com, the information you choose to send is used to respond and troubleshoot. Avoid including private reminder contents or credentials. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence; retention may be required for legitimate legal or operational reasons.

This website's optional analytics and hosting are separate from the Android app. See the website privacy policy.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact tripperdeelabs@gmail.com.
