Boss Button support

If B does not appear, move, open, or return as expected, send us the details and we will investigate.

Contact

Email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. Include your device model, Android version, app version, and what happened.

Send a support report

B does not appear

  • Confirm Display over other apps access remains enabled.
  • Return to Boss Button and tap Start the B button.
  • Check that the persistent Boss Button notification is present.
  • Review manufacturer battery or automatic-launch controls if needed.

Using the cover

  • Double tap B quickly to open the cover.
  • Drag B to move it; release to snap to the nearest edge.
  • Triple tap the cover to return.
  • Complete Android authentication if protected dismissal is enabled.

Custom image unavailable

The original document may have moved, been deleted, or lost provider access. Select it again; Boss Button otherwise falls back to its default cover.

Privacy and local data

Settings remain on your device. Remove them with Android's Clear storage control or by uninstalling. TripperDeeLabs cannot recover local settings.

Important limitation

Boss Button is a visual privacy cover, not a device lock or security boundary. Android system UI and secure surfaces may appear above it.
