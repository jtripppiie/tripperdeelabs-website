Moments Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Effective October 9, 2026

This policy covers Moments, a photo watch face for Wear OS developed by TripperDeeLabs (package com.tripperdeelabs.moments.wear). There is no separate TripperDeeLabs phone app.

Your photos

You choose personal photos through your watch's paired-phone companion software, which is provided by Google or your watch manufacturer, not by TripperDeeLabs. Wear OS and that companion handle photo selection, cropping, transfer from your phone to your watch over the paired connection, and storage on the watch. Moments only displays the photos you select on your watch. Your photos are never sent to TripperDeeLabs, and Moments has no way to upload them anywhere.

The face also includes illustrative AI-generated sample images (an alpine lake, a coastal sunset and a dog portrait). The alpine lake is shown until you select a photo.

Information displayed on your watch

The face displays the time, date, battery level and data from the complication providers you select, which may include steps, heart rate, sunrise and sunset times, and weather. Wear OS renders these values on the device. The watch face does not send them to TripperDeeLabs.

Collection, sharing and retention

Moments does not collect or share personal data off your device. It is built with Watch Face Format and contains no executable app code, advertising or analytics SDK, developer server, account system or network permission, and it requests no Android permissions. TripperDeeLabs does not receive, store or sell your photos or watch readings. Your photo choices and watch-face settings are managed locally by Wear OS.

Other services

Google Play handles purchases and distribution under Google's own privacy policy; TripperDeeLabs does not receive your payment-card information. Wear OS, your watch manufacturer, the paired-phone companion and your selected complication providers have their own permissions and privacy policies. A provider may use its own services, and tapping its complication may open its app. These services are separate from Moments.

Your choices

Use the watch-face editor to change or remove selected photos, switch to a sample image or a neutral panel, choose data providers, manage provider permissions in watch settings, or uninstall the face. Storage controls for transferred photos are managed by your watch and companion software. Uninstalling the face does not delete the original photos from your phone. Moments does not create an account, so no account-deletion process is required.

Support email

If you contact feedback@tripperdeelabs.com, we receive the information you voluntarily include and use it to respond. You can request deletion of that correspondence by emailing the same address. Please do not send private photos, sensitive health data or other unnecessary personal information.

This website

This policy describes the watch face. Visiting tripperdeelabs.com is covered separately by our website privacy policy. This app-policy page does not load analytics scripts.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact TripperDeeLabs at feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. This policy will be updated, with a new effective date, if the face's data practices change.
