Launch Vector Watch Face · Wear OS

Rocket launch countdown watch face for Wear OS

Launch Vector is a free Wear OS watch face with a rocket launch countdown. It combines time and everyday watch readings with launch status and an animated rocket. It is for space enthusiasts who want to glance at a scheduled launch from their wrist while still using the watch for ordinary timekeeping.

Free · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Add the launch-data companion

Install the free Launch Vector Data Provider companion so the face can refresh its schedule. The face and companion perform different jobs: one displays the countdown, while the other retrieves launch information. Fresh schedules require internet access. The companion keeps the latest successful schedule and refresh times on the watch, but a saved report cannot account for a schedule change while disconnected.

Read time and the countdown together

The face supports 12-hour and 24-hour time, launch status, an interactive display, and a quieter ambient mode. Its rocket animation is part of the visual design. Weather, battery, steps, and heart-rate fields display information supplied through Wear OS and compatible providers. Available data and update behavior depend on the watch, its settings, and the services selected for those fields.

Know where network requests go

Neither the watch face nor the data companion requests your device location. Launch-site locations refer to the rockets. The companion contacts the TripperDeeLabs launch service over HTTPS; that service and its hosting provider can process normal connection details such as IP addresses and request times. It does not receive your health readings, an advertising identifier, or an account profile. The apps contain no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Launch Vector free?

The watch face and Launch Vector Data Provider companion are free. Check Google Play for device compatibility and regional availability before installing.

Does the countdown update without internet?

Fresh launch schedules need a connection through the companion. Cached information can become outdated, so confirm an important launch with the launch operator before making plans.

Does this app track my location?

No. Neither app requests device location access. The launch service receives ordinary network metadata needed to deliver and operate its schedule service, as described in the privacy policy.

Is it an official SpaceX app?

No. Launch Vector is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SpaceX. Its launch-inspired design does not imply access to an operator’s internal schedule.
