Android puzzle game · Coming soon

Origins: Chain Reaction

Make room for your next move.

Drag glossy blocks onto an 8×8 board. Complete rows and columns, and use energy cores to clear the crossing line.

In development. A public Google Play release has not been announced.

Choose your challenge

Play Classic levels, a 90-second Timed Rush, five starter Puzzle Missions, Survival with extra blocks at tray refills, or an eight-line Quest. The daily calendar offers date-seeded boards.

Plan, rotate, recover

Rotate pieces freely. Each new run starts with two bombs for a 3×3 area and two zaps for a row-and-column cross. Your current run and best score stay saved on your device. Return with Continue, and pause timed play when you need a break.

Ads and purchases

The Android app includes interstitial ads between completed levels and an optional permanent Remove Ads purchase. The planned launch is free to play, with a $0.99 US one-time purchase to remove ads. Local prices and purchase availability will be shown by Google Play when released. Current development builds may use demo ads.
