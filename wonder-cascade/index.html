Wonder Cascade · Android

In development · Public Play listing unavailable

Match-3 puzzles with time to think

Wonder Cascade is a match-3 puzzle game in development for Android. Swap neighboring gems, clear gold, and use combinations of powers to reach the board’s goals. It is designed for players who enjoy thinking through a sequence of moves. The screenshots below show the development build, not a publicly released Google Play version.

Learn a move, then plan a combination

The opening lessons introduce matching three, creating a Lightning, and combining powers. Different match shapes create different effects: a line of four makes Lightning, a square makes a Glider, an L or T makes a Nova, and a line of five makes a Prism. Plan move lets you inspect the first wave of a chosen move before committing it.

Switch from cascades to finite puzzles

Regular journey boards introduce objectives such as gold, ice, suns, shields, and directional crystal relays. Every fifth fresh journey level is a Strategy Stop, with a finite board and no replacement gems. These challenges ask you to consider how later moves depend on the first. Free Undo lets you test an idea rather than starting over after every experiment.

A development preview with clear limits

The current build includes journey levels, expert puzzles, Mind puzzles, and daily challenges. There are no countdown timers or lives; settings include separate music and sound controls and Reduced Motion. Saved progress lets you return to a puzzle. Android advertising and purchase integration is still being tested, so this page does not promise an ad-free Android release or a final price. Read the app privacy policy for the current integration details.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download it from Google Play?

A public Google Play listing is not currently available at the known app URL. This page is a preview; a verified download link will be added when the app is released.

What makes Strategy Stops different?

They use finite gems with no replacements. Planning the order of swaps matters because one clear changes what remains for the next move.

Is there a timer?

The current development game has no countdown timers or lives. You can pause, return to saved puzzles, and use the available sound and motion settings.

Can I watch a puzzle being solved?

The development build includes Computer Run and solution viewing. Spectator runs keep personal records separate. Features may change before public release.
