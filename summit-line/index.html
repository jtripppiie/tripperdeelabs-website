Summit Line · Wear OS

Mountain watch face for Wear OS

Summit Line is a scenic digital watch face for Wear OS. Mountain backgrounds sit behind large time digits and everyday watch readings. It is for people who want an outdoor-inspired face for daily wear, with a choice of scenery and a step-goal ring rather than a full hiking or navigation app.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Choose scenery and readable time

Pick Sunrise Hike, Whitney Trail, Alpine, Night Trail, or Pure Black. Automatic scenery changes with fixed morning, daytime, and night periods based on local time; it does not calculate sunrise or sunset at your location. Choose an accent color, show or hide seconds and the moon phase, and select a supported time format. A minimal ambient display keeps time visible on compatible watch settings.

Set a goal for the step ring

The outer ring compares your watch’s native step count with a goal selected in Summit Line. Goals range from 5,000 to 20,000 steps, and the ring can be turned off. The number and ring use the same native count, but the selected goal does not synchronize with your health app. Summit Line displays available activity information rather than recording a workout itself.

Customize data without a studio account

Compatible complication slots can show information from installed providers. Available weather, heart rate, and other readings depend on the watch and those services. Summit Line has no ads, account, subscription, or developer-operated data service. On supported Wear OS 6 setups, the system photo-selection flow can also provide a personal background; the face does not upload that photo to TripperDeeLabs.

Frequently asked questions

Does Summit Line record hikes or show GPS routes?

No. The mountain artwork is decorative. The face has no built-in GPS navigation, hike recorder, compass service, or altitude service.

Which Wear OS version is required?

The face requires Wear OS 5 or newer with Watch Face Format support. Personal-photo customization needs Wear OS 6 and a supported watch and paired-phone system flow.

Can I use my health app’s step goal?

The ring uses native watch steps and a goal chosen inside Summit Line. It does not synchronize the goal from a separate health app.

Why does a data field show a dash?

A reading may be unavailable from the watch or chosen provider. Check the provider’s installation, settings, and permissions. Some updates, such as weather, may need a connection.
