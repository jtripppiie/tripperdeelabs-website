ClapFind

Clap once. Find your phone nearby.

ClapFind is a simple phone finder for the moments your Android phone is close by but out of sight. Turn listening on, put the phone down, and clap to make it ring, vibrate, and flash.

In development. A public Google Play listing is not available yet. The planned US price is $0.50 as a one-time download; the final listing price will be confirmed before release.

How it works

  1. Tap Start listening and allow microphone access.
  2. Place your phone nearby with the microphone unobstructed.
  3. Clap to trigger the phone’s alarm, vibration, and optional flashlight signal.

An ongoing Android notification stays visible while listening is on. Stop listening from the app or its notification. ClapFind only helps locate a phone within sound range; it is not a remote tracker.

Adjust it for your room

Choose high, medium, or low sensitivity. Softer claps may need high sensitivity; a noisy room may need low. Sudden sounds can sometimes be mistaken for a clap, so accuracy may vary with the room, phone placement, and device.

Audio stays on your phone

The prototype analyzes microphone samples on-device and does not save or upload audio. Listening starts only when you turn it on and stops when you turn it off. Camera access is used only for the optional flashlight alert. Read the privacy policy for details.
