FAMILYLINK OFFLINE CHAT · ANDROID · IN DEVELOPMENT

Stay in touch.
Stay nearby.

A small group chat for the people with you. One Android phone hosts a room; nearby adults join over Bluetooth or a local Wi-Fi hotspot, with host approval.

Built for camping trips, festivals and everyday time together when you want a local connection without another account or a mobile data plan.

A nearby room

One host and up to five guests. Join with a PIN or room invitation and wait for the host to approve you. The host can remove participants.

More than a message

Native-app chat includes text, small photos, editable own messages, short voice notes and local voice-call controls. Delivery labels distinguish local messages from messages received by the host.

Choose your view

Pick a profile color, switch between light and dark themes, adjust chat text size or use Simple mode with larger controls. A short tutorial explains joining.

Useful tools for the group.

  • Browser guests: a Wi-Fi host can offer a two-step invitation: join Wi-Fi, then open the chat webpage. Guests enter a name and wait for approval. Browser chat supports text and meeting acknowledgments, not native photos, voice or timed locations.
  • A meeting plan: the host can pin a meeting place, details, time and an optional photo. Members can acknowledge the current plan.
  • Optional locations: preview and send a location once, or explicitly start 30- or 60-minute updates for approved native-app members. Timed updates run about every five minutes and can continue with the screen locked. Stop in the app or notification. No silent tracking or movement history is provided.
  • Group tools: send an alert to this room and see acknowledgments, or use a Morse screen, flashlight or beep signal. These tools do not contact emergency services.
  • Local history: an encrypted, bounded recent conversation and reconnection information stay on each phone. Reopen an approved room when available, or delete saved data while disconnected.
  • Connection checking: native Wi-Fi members can compare seven security symbols in person for their current host connection. Reconnects require a fresh comparison.
  • Sharing and feedback: share a standalone installer where supported, or compose feedback with an optional, previewable diagnostic summary. Nothing is emailed automatically.

A preview, with clear limits.

NearCrew is being prepared for adults aged 18 and older: nearby friends, travel groups and campers. The app and browser ask for a local adult self-declaration, not verified identity. Social-app safety and privacy review remain part of release preparation. Two-device radio, call and timed-location testing remains part of release qualification. Screens and automated tests alone do not prove every feature works on every phone.

Everyone must remain within reach of the host. It is not a mesh network, internet messenger, remote location tracker or emergency-response service. Range, background restrictions, phone settings and interference affect delivery.

Private choices, explained plainly.

No NearCrew cloud account, advertising or analytics SDK is used. Native Wi-Fi connections use TLS and saved chat uses Android Keystore-backed encryption. The host relays and can read the room’s content; this is not end-to-end group encryption.

Optional browser chat uses local HTTP. Someone able to monitor or alter that network can read or change browser traffic. Prefer the native app for sensitive conversations. Saved history has limits: 80 entries, 10 photos, 10 voice notes and 10 queued messages. Starting or joining another room replaces the saved conversation.

NearCrew Offline Chat is an independent TripperDeeLabs project, not Google Family Link. It does not manage a child’s Google account or control their phone.
