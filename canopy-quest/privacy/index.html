Canopy Quest Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Updated October 5, 2026

Game data

Canopy Quest works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases. Scores, completed runs, collected relics, saved expeditions, and sound preferences stay on your device. The game does not transmit this information to TripperDeeLabs or other parties.

Storage and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are excluded. We do not hold server copies of your game progress.

Support email

If you email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com, your email provider sends us the address, message, and attachments you choose to provide. We use that correspondence to respond to your request. We retain support correspondence as needed to address your request and related follow-up; you can request deletion by email. Do not send sensitive personal information.

Google Play

Google Play handles purchases and downloads under Google's own policies. TripperDeeLabs does not receive your payment-card information from the game.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or support-email deletion requests, contact feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date.
