Tabletop Bingo

Big numbers, big buttons, and a bingo game for the whole table.

Play solo or host a Bluetooth game for nearby friends. Each player gets a card, the host calls numbers at your pace, and you cross off the squares you match. Large text sizes keep the numbers easy to read on phones and tablets.

In development. A public Google Play release has not been announced.

Play your way

Play a solo card on your phone, or host a Bluetooth game and let up to five nearby players join. The host approves each player before the game starts.

Easy to read

Choose from three text sizes. On a tablet in landscape, the card and called numbers sit side by side so the whole table can see the board.

Save your solo card

Solo games are saved on your device, so you can resume your card after closing the app.

Ads and purchases

Free play includes ads between completed rounds. Remove Ads is easy to find on Home, Settings and the results screen, with a planned one-time US price of $0.99 and no subscription. Google Play shows your local checkout price. Purchases and restoration will be verified through a Play test release before launch.
