Salmon Tracker: Kenai Counts · Android

Alaska salmon counts on your Android phone

Salmon Tracker brings public Alaska salmon passage counts to your Android phone. Follow selected Kenai Peninsula runs, view daily counts alongside season totals, and compare reports across years. It is for anglers and anyone following the progress of a salmon run who wants those reports together in a phone app.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Choose the rivers and runs you follow

Select the count projects that matter to you: Kenai River late-run sockeye, Kenai River kings, Kasilof River sockeye, and Russian River early- and late-run sockeye. The app retrieves public Alaska Department of Fish and Game reports. Reporting dates and available counts depend on the source; a new calendar day does not necessarily mean a new count has been published.

Read daily counts in context

Use daily and cumulative charts to look beyond a single number. Seven-day, fourteen-day, and season views let you examine different periods, while prior-season comparisons show how reported passage differs between years. Optional notifications can alert you to new counts or changes. Settings include quiet hours, synchronization controls, and a Wi-Fi-only option. You can also request a manual check for new counts.

Cached reports when coverage drops

An internet connection is needed to download updates. Reports already downloaded remain available when your connection drops, so check the reporting date before treating a cached number as current. Salmon Tracker has no ads or account requirement. Preferences and downloaded counts stay on your device. Requests go directly to the public data service, which may receive normal connection information such as your IP address.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official Alaska government app?

No. Salmon Tracker is an independent, unofficial client. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the State of Alaska or the Alaska Department of Fish and Game.

Can I read counts offline?

Yes, if they were downloaded previously. New reports, revisions, and fresh alerts require a connection; cached reports do not update while you are offline.

Do fish counts predict what I will catch?

No. Passage reports describe counted fish, not your likely catch. Consult official fishing regulations, emergency orders, and local conditions before fishing.

Do I have to enable notifications?

No. Notifications are optional. You can turn them off and open the app to check counts yourself. The US Play listing is a one-time $0.50 purchase, with regional prices shown by Google Play.
