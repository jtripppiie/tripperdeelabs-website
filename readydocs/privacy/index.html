ReadyDocs privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy covers the ReadyDocs Android app, package com.tripperdeelabs.readydocs, provided by TripperDeeLabs.

Documents and emergency information

ReadyDocs reads the PDFs or images you select in Android’s file picker and stores encrypted copies on your phone. Imported copies can retain information embedded in the original files. The optional emergency card stores the name, emergency contact details and medical notes you choose to enter. The app can display the files you select for a handoff and the separate card while Android reports that the phone is securely locked. Anyone who can see that screen can read the information displayed.

Optional card photos

The guided flow opens your phone’s camera app to capture the front and back of a card. ReadyDocs grants the camera access to a temporary destination inside ReadyDocs. The app removes its temporary photo after processing and re-encodes the image without copying the original camera metadata. If you save the pair, ReadyDocs stores it as one encrypted vault item with the generic card type. The camera app may handle photos under its own settings and policy.

Local processing and screen-lock verification

ReadyDocs stores vault copies in app-private storage using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Android Keystore manages the encryption key. Android performs the screen-lock verification used for owner setup. ReadyDocs receives the verification result without receiving your PIN, pattern or password. ReadyDocs uses Android’s secure-window controls to block ordinary screenshots and screen recording of the viewer, but a person can still photograph the display with another device.

Optional automatic lock and shortcuts

If you opt in through Android Settings, ReadyDocs uses Android’s force-lock device administrator capability when you start an automatic document handoff or tap the emergency Quick Settings tile while your phone is unlocked. You can disable it in owner setup or in Android’s device admin settings. The emergency widget and tile display a generic label and open the separate card only while the phone is securely locked. They do not include document or card content in their label or preview. You can also opt in to a quiet emergency notification. Its generic title and shortcut contain no saved data. ReadyDocs can restore the notification after a restart if you left it enabled, after Android makes the app storage available.

Network access and providers

The app requests no network permission and includes no account, advertising, analytics, payment feature, developer-operated sync or automatic crash reporting. TripperDeeLabs receives no vault contents or emergency-card entries from the app. If you select a file from a cloud-backed document provider, that provider handles the original under its own policy. ReadyDocs leaves the original in place.

Retention and deletion

Encrypted copies and the emergency card remain in ReadyDocs until you replace or remove them. You can do that in owner setup. Clearing app storage or uninstalling ReadyDocs removes the local vault. The app excludes vault data from Android backup and has no remote recovery service. Removing a ReadyDocs copy leaves originals and other external copies in place.

Children and families

ReadyDocs is intended for families, including children with help from a parent or guardian. ReadyDocs does not ask for a birth date or create a child profile. The same local handling applies to all ages. Parents and guardians can review what a child stores and remove it using the phone’s owner setup. Only share documents and emergency information that you have permission to show.

Support and website

If you email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com, we receive the details you send and use them to respond. Leave documents, medical details and device credentials out of support messages. This site’s data practices are described in the website privacy policy.
