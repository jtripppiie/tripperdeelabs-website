App privacy policyHelp and support

Privacy is the purpose

Choose which personal files to keep private and when to export a copy. Vault files, item details and stored thumbnails use AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. The developer does not receive your vault contents or hold a recovery key.

Import, capture and organize

Import selected files with Android's file picker, browse daily case folders, and view supported photos and videos inside the vault. The optional private camera captures photos and short, silent videos directly into the vault.

Keep control of your copies

Importing creates a protected copy. It does not automatically hide or delete originals from Gallery, Downloads or another app. Exported copies are unencrypted and outside vault protection.

Know before you start

Android 13 or later and a device screen lock are required. Imports are limited to 8 MiB per file; camera video stops at 60 seconds or 44 MiB. Hardware protection and camera capabilities vary by phone.

There is no cloud backup or recovery service. Lost patterns, devices or device keys can permanently prevent access. Keep separate, secure copies of irreplaceable files. The app has not been independently certified secure.

Production blocks screenshots through Android controls. Store Test is a separate testing app with screenshots enabled; its vault does not transfer automatically to production.
