Bluetooth Drag Racing

Build it. Tune it. Race it.

A retro-inspired, side-on drag racer built around quick runs, clean shifts and a garage that feels like yours. Rev at the lights, launch on green and carry your speed through the finish.

Your next tenth of a second

Find your pace

Run the quarter mile or half mile. Choose Rookie, Street or Pro computer opponents, or race a translucent replay of your own recorded best run.

Build your garage

Five fictional cars span classic muscle, street tuners and sports coupes. Rotate the garage view, choose paint and wheel finishes, and keep a separate build for every car.

Make upgrades count

Earn race credits for tires, engines, transmissions, nitrous, turbo and weight reduction. Adjust final-drive gearing and compare simulated acceleration in the dyno shop.

Built for the race

Actual Android development-build captures. The interface and features may change before release.

Race a friend nearby

Bluetooth Host and Join are built in for paired Android devices. Both players need the same app version. Physical two-device race and connection acceptance is still being verified; Bluetooth is not yet a verified release feature.

Six places to put your build to work

Race the Drag Strip, Dirt Road, Downtown, Waterfront, Desert Highway or Night Airfield. Choose dry, damp or rainy conditions. Surface grip, wheelspin, dust and wet-road spray affect the experience. Compare a saved build against your current tuning in the dyno shop.

Play on your terms

Solo races, garage upgrades, records and ghosts work offline without an account. Choose manual or automatic shifting, left- or right-handed controls, MPH or KPH, and optional engine audio. The current release candidate includes optional Google Play checkout and ads for eligible adults after a successful privacy check. Earned-credit unlocks remain available offline. The planned launch is free with optional purchases and ads only between completed races; under-18 and unspecified-age players receive no ads. The planned one-time paint, gold-wheel and Apex GT purchases are US$0.99 each, with earned-credit alternatives. Google Play availability is not yet announced.

This is an arcade drag racer. The dyno shows the game’s simulated acceleration, not a real-world horsepower measurement. Ghosts are saved by car, race distance and weather/surface conditions; finish a computer run first to create one.
