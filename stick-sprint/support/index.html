Stick Sprint support

Stick Sprint is in development for Google Play.

Controls

Tap either side of the playfield to change lanes. Swipe up to jump; swipe down to slide or descend while airborne. Use Pause to take a break. Sound, volume, swipe sensitivity and run theme are available in Settings.

Your progress

Progress is saved locally. Avoid uninstalling or clearing app storage while troubleshooting because that can erase your scores and unlocks.

Report a problem

Email feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. Include your device model, Android version, game version, run theme and steps to reproduce the problem. Attach a screenshot if useful, and avoid sharing sensitive information.
