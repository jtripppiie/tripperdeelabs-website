ClapFind · Android · In development

Privacy policy

Updated November 22, 2026

Publisher: TripperDeeLabs · Contact: tripperdeelabs@gmail.com

Microphone

ClapFind requests microphone permission to detect a clap while listening is enabled. The Android app analyzes short audio samples in memory on your device. It does not save raw audio recordings, send audio to a server, or upload microphone samples. Listening starts only after you turn it on and stops when you turn it off, use Stop in the ongoing notification, or Android ends the service. Clap detection requires the phone to be powered on and within microphone range.

Camera and flashlight

The app may request camera permission to flash the phone’s built-in torch after a clap. The camera is not used to take photos or video. If camera permission is not granted or a flashlight is unavailable, the sound and vibration alerts can still work.

Settings and other data

The current prototype stores the selected clap-sensitivity level and listening state locally on the device. It does not require an account and contains no ads, analytics SDK, or internet permission. No personal information is sent to TripperDeeLabs.

Device controls

You can revoke microphone or camera permission in Android Settings. Turn listening off in ClapFind or use the Stop action in its notification. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local settings.

Children and changes

ClapFind is a nearby phone-finding utility and does not ask for a birth date or account. This policy may change if the app’s features or included software change. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised date.
