Parked: Find My Car Widget · Android

Find where I parked my car

Parked is an Android parking reminder for the moment you leave your car and the moment you need to find it again. Save one location from the app or its home-screen widget, then open walking directions back to that spot. It is useful for unfamiliar streets, shopping trips, airport lots, and busy event parking.

$0.50 USD · US Play listing checked 2026-10-26. Regional price and availability may vary.

Save your spot before you walk away

Tap Park Here while you are near the car and allow foreground location access. Parked records the coordinates, save time, and accuracy reported by Android. The widget puts the parking controls on your home screen, so you do not need to navigate through the app each time. Check the saved accuracy before relying on the pin.

Return, share, or replace your parking pin

When you are ready to return, use the find-car control to send the saved destination to your chosen maps app. You can also share a map link with someone meeting you at the car. A new parking location replaces the previous one, and the app includes a control to clear it. Parked remembers a single parking spot rather than keeping a history of your journeys.

What stays on your phone

Saving the pin uses the phone’s location services and does not require internet access. Parked has no account, ads, analytics, background location access, or internet permission. TripperDeeLabs cannot retrieve your saved location. Choosing directions or sharing passes the destination to another app, whose connectivity needs and privacy practices apply. GPS reception and accuracy can be limited by buildings, particularly inside parking garages.

Frequently asked questions

Does Parked work without a signal?

You can save a location without internet when your phone can obtain a location fix. Walking directions open in your maps app and may need a connection or previously downloaded maps.

Does it remember my parking level?

The saved pin is a geographic location. In a multi-level garage, make a separate note of the floor or bay; GPS alone may not distinguish levels.

Does it track my car automatically?

No. Tap to save your spot when you park. Parked does not request background location access and is not a vehicle tracker.

Is Parked a subscription?

No. The US Google Play listing offers a one-time $0.50 purchase. Check Google Play for the price and availability in your region.
