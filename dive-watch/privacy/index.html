Dive Watch Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Effective October 9, 2026

This policy covers Dive Watch, a watch face for Wear OS developed by TripperDeeLabs (package com.tripperdeelabs.arcember.wear). There is no separate phone app.

Information displayed on your watch

The face displays the time, date, battery level and data from the complication provider you select, such as step count. Wear OS renders these values on the device. The watch face does not send them to TripperDeeLabs.

Collection, sharing and retention

Dive Watch does not collect or share personal data off your device. It is built with Watch Face Format and contains no executable app code, advertising or analytics SDK, developer server, account system or network permission, and it requests no Android permissions. TripperDeeLabs does not receive or retain your watch readings. Watch-face customization is managed locally by Wear OS.

Other services

Google Play handles purchases and distribution under Google's own privacy policy; TripperDeeLabs does not receive your payment-card information. Wear OS, your watch manufacturer and your selected complication providers have their own permissions and privacy policies. A provider may use its own services, and tapping its complication may open its app. These services are separate from Dive Watch.

Your choices

Use the watch-face editor to choose a data provider, manage provider permissions in watch settings, or uninstall the face. Dive Watch does not create an account, so no account-deletion process is required.

Support email

If you contact feedback@tripperdeelabs.com, we receive the information you voluntarily include and use it to respond. You can request deletion of that correspondence by emailing the same address. Please do not send sensitive health data or other unnecessary personal information.

This website

This policy describes the watch face. Visiting tripperdeelabs.com is covered separately by our website privacy policy. This app-policy page does not load analytics scripts.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact TripperDeeLabs at feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. This policy will be updated, with a new effective date, if the face's data practices change.
