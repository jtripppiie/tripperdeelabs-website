Mock Location: Fake GPS

Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

The short version: Mock Location: Fake GPS has no advertising SDK, account system, first-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or developer-operated location server. Local settings and saved locations stay on your device. Map display and address search use the network services described below.

Who publishes the app

Mock Location: Fake GPS is developed and published by TripperDeeLabs. Privacy questions may be sent to TripperDeeLabs support.

Data stored on the device

The app stores the following information in its private Android application storage:

  • Up to five bookmark names and coordinates
  • The last selected location and label
  • The last map camera position
  • Active-session settings and status

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this application. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its private local data.

Network services

The app needs internet access for map tiles and address search. TripperDeeLabs does not operate the servers used for these functions.

Map display

MapLibre renders OpenStreetMap-derived map data provided by OpenFreeMap. OpenFreeMap receives the normal network information needed to return map resources, such as the device IP address and requested tile or style URLs. The app does not add an advertising ID, account ID, or developer-controlled tracking identifier to those requests.

Address search

Address and place text entered into the search field is sent to the Photon service operated by komoot. Photon returns matching OpenStreetMap locations. A request may include the search text, language, an approximate map-bias coordinate, the device IP address, and standard network metadata. Search responses are cached temporarily in memory to reduce repeated requests and are not uploaded to TripperDeeLabs.

You can avoid address search by selecting a point directly on the map or entering latitude and longitude coordinates.

Physical location permission

Location permission is requested when you start a mock-location session because Google Play services requires it to enable fused mock-location compatibility. Starting a session does not cause this app to read your device's physical location. The optional current-device-location button reads the most recent physical position available from Android locally to move the map and select a point. TripperDeeLabs does not receive or store physical or simulated locations on a server.

Mock-location sessions

Selected and simulated coordinates are generated locally through Android's official mock-location testing capability. The app does not attempt to conceal Android's mock-location indicator or bypass applications that reject mock locations.

Permissions

  • Internet: loads map resources and performs Photon address searches.
  • Location: enables Google Play services fused mock-location compatibility when a session starts and selects the current device position only when you invoke that optional feature.
  • Notifications: displays active-session controls on supported Android versions.
  • Foreground service: maintains a user-started mock-location test session with a persistent notification.
  • Mock location declaration: allows Android Developer Options to list the app as a selectable mock-location application.

The app does not request contacts, storage, camera, microphone, phone, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi scanning, advertising ID, or background physical-location access.

Children

The app is a developer and testing utility and is not directed to children.

Optional feedback diagnostics

When you tap Send feedback, the app prepares an editable email to TripperDeeLabs support containing the app and Android versions, device manufacturer and model, mock-location setup result, and whether a session is active. It excludes physical and simulated coordinates, bookmarks, search text, saved labels, and unique device identifiers. Nothing is sent automatically.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by updating the effective date and this published page.
