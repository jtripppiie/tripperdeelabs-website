Kenai Report Data

Kenai Fish Count and Tide Complications for Wear OS

Kenai Report Data supplies public fishing-report data to compatible Wear OS complications. It is a separate watch app, not a watch face, and can work with Kenai Fishing Report or another compatible face.

It fetches ADF&G counts, USGS readings and NOAA tide predictions over the internet and caches validated reports. Requires Wear OS 5 or newer. Check official rules and conditions; this independent app does not decide whether fishing is permitted.

Free on the US Play listing · Checked October 23, 2026. Prices and availability may vary by region.

Features

  • Kenai late-run sockeye counts
  • Soldotna water readings
  • Cape Kasilof tide predictions
  • Report times, refresh and official source links
