Neon Air Hockey privacy policy

Updated November 6, 2026. This policy describes the current Android development build, package com.tripperdeelabs.neonairhockey, provided by TripperDeeLabs.

Local game data

Scores, your saved local match, difficulty and sound preferences are stored in the app on your device. There is no TripperDeeLabs account or cloud-save service. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes this data. The app disables Android backup through its manifest; device transfer behavior may vary by Android version and manufacturer.

Optional Bluetooth

Bluetooth play uses Android’s paired devices and Nearby devices permission on supported Android versions. It sends live match state and mallet movements directly to your opponent. The app does not upload those match messages to a TripperDeeLabs server. Leaving the app ends the connection. Local games do not need Bluetooth permission.

Advertising

The development build uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google test interstitials between completed matches. Production ads are disabled. The SDK communicates with Google and may process IP addresses, device information, diagnostics and ad interactions for ad delivery, security and measurement. Current requests use G-rated, child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment flags. These settings are not a claim that a production Families release has been approved. No developer-operated analytics SDK is included.

See Google’s privacy policy. Production advertising, audience and consent configuration must be completed before release, and this policy will be updated if practices change.

Permissions and choices

Internet and network-state access support ads. The app does not request location, camera, microphone or contacts access. You can decline Nearby devices permission and continue local play. There are no purchases in the current build.

Support

If you email tripperdeelabs@gmail.com, we receive the information you send and use it to respond. Avoid sending passwords or payment details. Contact that address with privacy questions or requests concerning support correspondence.

Website analytics are separate from the Android game and covered by the website privacy policy.
